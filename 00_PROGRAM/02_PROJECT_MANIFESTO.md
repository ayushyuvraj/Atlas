# Project Manifesto

## Purpose

This project exists to create a flagship Enterprise AI product and the operating trail behind it.

The goal is not merely to build an app. The goal is to simulate how a senior product leader would discover, define, design, build, validate, launch, and communicate a serious enterprise AI product.

The repository should help a reviewer understand:

- Why this problem was chosen.
- Why this product direction was selected.
- What alternatives were considered.
- How major decisions were made.
- How AI is central to the product.
- How the product could become a broader enterprise platform.
- How the work demonstrates senior AI product leadership.

## North Star

An experienced product, engineering, business, or AI leader should be able to review the repository and see:

- Clear product judgment.
- Systems thinking.
- Customer understanding.
- Technical fluency.
- Communication discipline.
- Execution rigor.

The work should demonstrate that standard through artifacts, decisions, and traceable reasoning rather than claims about the author.

## Product We Are Building

The product direction is frozen:

- **Platform Vision:** Enterprise Decision Operating System
- **Category:** Enterprise Intelligence Platform
- **Core Product:** AI-Native Multi-Agent Decision Studio
- **Initial Wedge:** Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support
- **Core Innovation:** Institutional Reasoning Engine
- **Management Framework:** Institutional Reasoning Management

## Operating Belief

Organizations do not only need more dashboards, copilots, or search tools. They need a better way to reason through high-stakes decisions, preserve the rationale behind those decisions, and learn from outcomes over time.

## Product Philosophy

AI does not replace executive judgment. It makes executive judgment more rigorous, transparent, and repeatable.

The product exists to improve human decision-making, not to remove human accountability.

## Why Documentation Matters

Documentation is not administrative overhead. In this project, documentation is how product reasoning, decisions, tradeoffs, and learning are preserved.

Every artifact should answer a specific question:

- Opportunity documents answer: Why this problem?
- Strategy documents answer: Why this product?
- Customer documents answer: Why these users and buyers?
- Decision science documents answer: Why this reasoning model?
- Product system documents answer: How does the product work?
- PRD documents answer: What exactly are we building?
- Architecture documents answer: How can this be built credibly?
- Launch documents answer: How does the product communicate value?
- Professional narrative documents answer: How should this work be communicated clearly and responsibly?

## How We Work

Every major milestone follows the same lifecycle:

1. Plan
2. Research
3. Create
4. Review
5. Revise
6. Freeze
7. Update traceability documents
8. Continue

## Review Standard

Every important artifact should be reviewed from multiple perspectives:

- Product leadership
- Enterprise customer
- Engineering
- Design
- AI
- Security
- Business
- Executive leadership
- Professional narrative
- Skeptical critic

The purpose of review is not to defend the work. The purpose is to strengthen it.

## Integrity Rule

The repository must distinguish between:

- Validated facts
- Hypotheses
- Strategic assumptions
- Illustrative examples
- Future validation work

We will not claim customer research, production usage, or professional experience that did not happen.

## Definition of Success

This project succeeds when:

1. The product direction is coherent and differentiated.
2. Every major decision is traceable.
3. The repository reads like the working system of a real product organization.
4. The MVP and launch assets make the product understandable without explanation.
5. The professional narrative assets make the product journey understandable outside the working team.
