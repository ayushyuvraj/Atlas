# Research Library

## Purpose

This library will store external sources, internal notes, and research summaries used to support product decisions.

At this stage, the repository is being populated from the prior chat and frozen program direction. Formal external research will be added in later research milestones.

## Integrity Rule

Do not treat unsourced claims as validated research. Until a claim is supported by evidence, label it as a hypothesis or working assumption.
