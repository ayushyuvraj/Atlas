## 1. Capability Objective

This matrix translates senior Enterprise AI product leadership standards into concrete operating capabilities.

The project is not trying to claim a title. It is designed to create a rigorous body of work that shows product judgment, structure, and systems thinking through artifacts.

---

## 2. Current Evidence Summary

Local source inputs and prior project summaries already show strong proof points in several areas:

- Enterprise AI product strategy in regulated banking environments.
- Customer discovery and workflow reframing.
- Product judgment across RAG, multimodal AI, dynamic friction, governance, and trust.
- Executive stakeholder management with CIO/CRO/CFO/MD-level stakeholders.
- Cross-functional delivery and enterprise transformation leadership.
- Commercial contribution through business cases, RFPs, ROI models, and client-funded initiatives.
- Responsible AI instincts around auditability, privacy, explainability, governance, and human accountability.

The flagship project should therefore not repeat the same BFSI story. It should extend the story into a horizontal, cross-industry Enterprise AI platform.

## 4. Priority Capabilities To Build Through Flagship

The highest-priority development areas for this project are:

1. Agentic AI and multi-agent product strategy.
2. AI evaluation and reasoning quality measurement.
3. Technical architecture depth.
4. Cross-industry Enterprise AI positioning.
5. Launch and market presence.
6. Thought leadership system.
7. Public-facing product narrative.

These are not weaknesses in the underlying experience. They are the areas where the flagship program must create the clearest product, technical, and operating artifacts.

---

## 5. Capability-to-Artifact Rule

Every future artifact must answer at least one of these questions:

- Which senior product capability does this demonstrate?
- Which operating capability does this strengthen?
- Which public product narrative use case does this support?
- Which product decision does this make more credible?

If an artifact does none of these, it should not be created.
