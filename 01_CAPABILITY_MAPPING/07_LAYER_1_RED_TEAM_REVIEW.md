## Red Team Review

### Product Leadership

**Assessment:** The capability matrix correctly separates current evidence from flagship evidence. This prevents the project from becoming generic documentation.

**Concern:** It should not over-index on internal narrative optics at the expense of product substance.

**Response:** The matrix ties each operating signal to a concrete product artifact.

### Senior AI Product Leadership Lens

**Assessment:** The capability development plan correctly identifies agentic AI, AI evaluation, technical architecture, cross-industry positioning, and launch evidence as priority gaps.

**Concern:** AI evaluation and agentic architecture need to become deep artifacts later, not surface-level language.

**Response:** These are explicitly marked as high-priority future evidence.

### Enterprise Customer

**Assessment:** The matrix recognizes that enterprise credibility already exists through regulated banking work.

**Concern:** The flagship must avoid becoming too abstract.

**Response:** The Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support wedge remains the anchor.

### Engineering

**Assessment:** Technical fluency is correctly treated as a development area requiring architecture artifacts.

**Concern:** The current matrix does not prove technical depth yet.

**Response:** Correct. Layer 1 identifies the development area; Layer 9 will address it.

### UX / Design

**Assessment:** Product design artifacts are correctly flagged as medium-priority development area.

**Concern:** UX should not be treated as a cosmetic layer.

**Response:** Layer 8 includes flows, interaction patterns, accessibility, and design system.

### GTM / Business

**Assessment:** Business strategy and launch development areas are clearly identified.

**Concern:** Pricing/GTM could be deferred too late if the product definition becomes too product-heavy.

**Response:** Layer 10 is explicitly dedicated to commercial viability.

### Professional Narrative

**Assessment:** The current proof base is strong but BFSI-heavy. The flagship correctly expands the narrative horizontally.

**Concern:** Avoid claiming title equivalence directly.

**Response:** The documents use evidence-led positioning rather than title claims.

### Skeptical Critic

**Assessment:** Layer 1 is structurally complete.

**Concern:** The capability benchmark is based on inferred public role signals rather than internal product leadership rubrics.

**Response:** This is acceptable for now but should become an optional future research artifact.
