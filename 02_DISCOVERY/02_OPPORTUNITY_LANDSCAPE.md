## Starting Question

The discovery process began with the question:

> What enterprise AI problem should become the flagship product that demonstrates senior Enterprise AI Product Leadership?

The goal was not to find a clever AI feature. The goal was to identify a meaningful enterprise problem that could support:

- Product vision
- Customer discovery
- AI-native architecture
- Strategic prioritization
- Enterprise adoption
- GTM and monetization
- Technical credibility
- Public product storytelling and stakeholder communication

## Strategic Lenses Used

The domains were reviewed through twelve lenses:

1. CEO frustrations
2. Enterprise CIO priorities
3. Product leader pain points
4. AI research trends
5. Future of work
6. Regulatory shifts
7. Knowledge management
8. Decision intelligence
9. Agentic AI
10. Organizational behavior
11. Platform economics
12. Startup disruption opportunities

---

## Emerging Pattern

The strongest opportunity spaces converged around one theme:

> Enterprises do not need more disconnected AI assistants. They need better organizational reasoning.

This insight moved the project away from generic chatbots, dashboards, and search tools and toward decision intelligence, institutional memory, multi-perspective reasoning, and enterprise decision workflows.

---

## Shortlist

The strongest candidates were:

1. Enterprise AI Operating System
2. Executive Decision Intelligence
3. Enterprise Intelligence Layer
4. Organizational Memory Graph
5. Business Simulation and Scenario Intelligence
6. AI Agent Workforce Platform

---

## Outcome

The opportunity landscape led to the final platform direction:

**Enterprise Decision Operating System**

with the first product:

**AI-Native Multi-Agent Decision Studio**

and initial wedge:

**Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support**
