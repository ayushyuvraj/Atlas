# Jobs To Be Done Exploration

## Document Status

**Layer:** 2 - Opportunity Discovery  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Status:** Frozen  
**Source:** Reconstructed from the prior Flagship Project chat.  
**Purpose:** Translate customer pain into Jobs To Be Done for the selected opportunity.

---

## 1. JTBD Framing

The discovery process reframed the customer problem from:

> "Executives need AI answers."

to:

> "Enterprise teams need a better way to prepare, challenge, decide, document, and learn from strategic decisions."

This distinction matters because the product is not a chatbot. It is a decision workflow and institutional reasoning system.

---

## 2. Primary Job

When enterprise leaders are preparing for a high-stakes strategic portfolio or investment decision, they want to:

> Compare options rigorously, align stakeholders, make a defensible decision, and preserve the reasoning so the organization can learn from the outcome.

---

## 3. Functional Jobs

| Job | Current Alternative | Product Opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame the decision clearly | Meeting agendas, slides, emails | Guided decision framing |
| Gather relevant context | Manual research, dashboards, document search | AI-assisted context assembly |
| Compare competing initiatives | Excel, PowerPoint, executive debate | Structured portfolio comparison |
| Surface assumptions | Manual review, expert judgment | Assumption extraction and challenge |
| Evaluate scenarios | Spreadsheet models, consulting decks | Scenario simulation and tradeoff analysis |
| Align stakeholders | Meetings and follow-ups | Shared decision workspace |
| Document rationale | Slides, minutes, emails | Immutable decision record |
| Learn from outcomes | Retrospectives, often skipped | Outcome review and institutional learning |

---

## 4. Emotional Jobs

Users want to feel:

- Prepared before executive meetings
- Confident in their recommendations
- Trusted by senior stakeholders
- Less exposed to hidden assumptions
- More in control of complex strategic decisions

---

## 5. Social Jobs

Users want to be seen as:

- Strategic thinkers
- Strong operators
- Evidence-led advisors
- Leaders who improve executive decision quality
- People who can bring order to complex cross-functional problems

---

## 6. Job Success Criteria

The product succeeds if users can say:

- "We reached alignment faster."
- "We understood the tradeoffs clearly."
- "We knew which assumptions mattered."
- "We could explain why this decision was made."
- "We learned from the outcome instead of losing the reasoning."

---

## 7. Rejected Jobs

The prior chat rejected several weaker jobs:

| Rejected Job | Why Rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Chat with enterprise documents | Too generic and crowded |
| Summarize meetings | Too tactical for senior product leadership signal |
| Manage AI agents | Infrastructure-heavy without clear buyer workflow |
| Build banking AI assistant | Too narrow and over-indexed on existing proof points |
| Create executive chatbot | Risk of becoming a generic assistant |

---

## 8. Red Team Review

### Product Leadership

**Challenge:** Are these jobs specific enough for MVP scope?

**Response:** The MVP should focus on strategic portfolio and investment decision support, not all decision types.

### Daily User

**Challenge:** Strategy teams already use PowerPoint and Excel. Why switch?

**Response:** The value is not replacing slides. It is reducing fragmented preparation, surfacing assumptions, and preserving reusable decision rationale.

### Product Narrative

**Challenge:** Does this show senior product operating discipline?

**Response:** Yes. It demonstrates customer problem decomposition, JTBD thinking, prioritization, and workflow design.

### Integrity

**Challenge:** These jobs are hypotheses.

**Response:** They should be validated later through user interviews or expert review before being treated as market evidence.
