## Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Why It Matters |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Size of customer pain | 15 | The product must solve a meaningful enterprise problem |
| AI-native advantage | 15 | AI must be central, not a superficial feature |
| Product leadership signal | 15 | The work must demonstrate senior product judgment |
| Enterprise differentiation | 10 | The product must avoid crowded generic AI categories |
| Public demo clarity | 10 | The product must be understandable and compelling in a live product walkthrough |
| Technical feasibility | 10 | MVP must be buildable within realistic constraints |
| Startup/platform potential | 10 | The idea should plausibly grow into a company/platform |
| GTM and monetization | 5 | There must be a buyer and budget logic |
| Cross-industry scalability | 5 | The product should not be limited to BFSI |
| Product narrative alignment | 5 | The idea should extend credibility without breaking it |

Total: 100

---

## Candidate Scoring Summary

| Candidate | Score | Key Strength | Key Concern |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Enterprise AI Operating System | 97 | Broad platform potential | Too broad as first MVP |
| Executive Decision Intelligence | 96 | Strong demo and executive relevance | Risk of becoming a generic assistant |
| Enterprise Intelligence Layer | 96 | Strong horizontal category potential | Too abstract without a wedge |
| AI Agent Workforce Platform | 95 | Strong agentic AI relevance | Risk of generic agent management |
| Business Simulation Platform | 92 | Strong scenario and reasoning value | Could narrow into planning software |
| Organizational Memory Graph | 91 | Defensible institutional memory | Could look like enterprise knowledge search |

---

## Interpretation

The highest raw score was Enterprise AI Operating System, but it was rejected as the first product because it was a vision rather than a buildable, demoable MVP.

The winning direction combined the best parts of the strongest candidates:

- Enterprise AI Operating System -> long-term platform ambition
- Executive Decision Intelligence -> executive workflow and demo clarity
- Enterprise Intelligence Layer -> horizontal category framing
- Organizational Memory Graph -> institutional memory
- Business Simulation Platform -> scenario reasoning
- AI Agent Workforce Platform -> multi-agent reasoning

---

## Final Prioritization Decision

The selected direction:

**Enterprise Decision Operating System**

The selected initial product:

**AI-Native Multi-Agent Decision Studio**

The selected first wedge:

**Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support**

---

## Tradeoff

The chosen idea is more complex than a narrow point solution, but it creates a stronger leadership signal because it demonstrates:

- Platform thinking
- AI-native product design
- Enterprise workflow understanding
- Strategic decision-making
- Cross-industry applicability
