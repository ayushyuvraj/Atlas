**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen

**Purpose:** Define the durable product identity, beliefs, principles, non-goals, and strategic guardrails for Atlas.

---

## Product Codename

**Atlas**

Atlas is the working product identity for the Enterprise Decision Operating System program.

---

## Vision

Create the operating layer that enables organizations to make consistently better strategic decisions through AI-assisted institutional reasoning.

---

## Mission

Empower enterprises to preserve, improve, and scale organizational decision-making using trustworthy AI.

---

## Long-Term Aspiration

Become for enterprise decision-making what CRM became for customer relationships and ERP became for business operations.

---

## Core Belief

Organizations do not only lose knowledge. They lose the reasoning behind their most important decisions.

That reasoning is rarely captured, challenged, reused, or improved over time.

---

## Customer Problem

Large organizations often have:

- Too much information
- Too many dashboards
- Too many documents
- Too many disconnected AI tools
- Too many high-stakes decisions prepared through informal processes

The bottleneck is not only information access. The bottleneck is organizational reasoning.

---

## Product Philosophy

- AI augments executive judgment rather than replacing it.
- Humans remain accountable for strategic decisions.
- Disagreement improves decision quality when structured constructively.
- Transparency builds trust.
- Organizational learning compounds over time.
- Every major decision should become a reusable institutional asset.

---

## Product Principles

1. **Decision First**
   Every feature must improve the quality, speed, traceability, or accountability of a strategic decision.

2. **Human Accountability**
   The platform recommends and explains. Enterprise leaders decide and remain accountable.

3. **Explainability by Default**
   Recommendations must expose evidence, assumptions, tradeoffs, confidence, and alternatives.

4. **Structured Disagreement**
   The product should surface disagreement before consensus, because hidden disagreement creates decision risk.

5. **Institutional Learning**
   Every completed decision should improve future decisions through outcome review and reusable memory.

6. **Cross-Industry Design**
   The product is designed around decision complexity rather than one industry.

7. **Trust Over Automation**
   Automating the wrong recommendation faster is not success. Trustworthy reasoning is the product.

---

## Jobs To Be Done

When enterprise teams face complex strategic decisions, help them:

- Frame the decision clearly
- Gather relevant evidence
- Challenge assumptions
- Surface blind spots
- Compare alternatives
- Evaluate tradeoffs
- Align stakeholders
- Document rationale
- Track outcomes
- Learn from previous decisions

---

## Non-Goals

Atlas is not intended to be:

- A generic chatbot
- A generic enterprise search product
- A BI dashboard
- A meeting summarizer
- A knowledge base
- A generic agent framework
- A workflow automation product without reasoning depth

These products may remain adjacent categories, but they do not define Atlas.

---

## Decision

Freeze Product DNA v1.0 as the product's strategic identity and decision filter.

Future product requirements, user journeys, design decisions, architecture, and GTM claims should trace back to these principles.
