---

## Product Wedge

The initial product wedge is:

> Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support

This wedge focuses on the recurring executive forums where organizations decide which initiatives, products, markets, transformations, or investments deserve funding and attention.

---

## Why This Wedge

Strategic portfolio and investment decisions are strong first workflows because they are:

- High value
- Cross-functional
- Recurring
- Politically complex
- Evidence-heavy
- Prone to assumption gaps
- Common across industries
- Naturally connected to executive decision forums

They also create a credible path from one workflow to a broader Decision Operating System.

---

## "I Need This Now" Trigger

The strongest trigger occurs before or during a strategic investment or portfolio review:

- Dozens of initiatives are competing for limited budget.
- Finance, product, operations, technology, and risk teams disagree.
- Teams spend weeks preparing decks and reconciling assumptions.
- The executive meeting exposes disagreement but does not create clear alignment.
- Decision rationale is poorly captured after the meeting.

This is the practical buying moment:

> The organization needs a better way to prepare, reason about, commit to, and learn from strategic investment decisions.

---

## MVP Thesis

The MVP should help a strategy, transformation, or portfolio team run one strategic investment review from intake to recommendation.

The MVP does not need to solve every enterprise decision. It needs to prove that Atlas can make one high-stakes decision workflow more structured, explainable, and reusable.

---

## MVP Workflow

1. Create a strategic decision workspace.
2. Frame the decision and desired outcome.
3. Add initiatives, alternatives, constraints, and stakeholders.
4. Assemble context and evidence.
5. Surface assumptions and risks.
6. Compare scenarios and tradeoffs.
7. Generate an executive recommendation.
8. Record decision rationale and next review date.
9. Preserve the decision as institutional memory.

---

## Initial Use Cases

| Use Case | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Investment committee preparation | Prepare evidence, assumptions, options, and recommendations before an executive meeting |
| Portfolio prioritization | Compare initiatives competing for limited resources |
| Transformation governance | Evaluate which programs deserve continued funding or escalation |
| Product portfolio review | Decide where to invest, pause, sunset, or accelerate |
| Capital allocation review | Connect strategy, financial constraints, and execution feasibility |

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative Wedge | Reason Not Selected First |
| --- | --- |
| Annual strategy planning | Important, but episodic and broad |
| AI transformation prioritization | Timely, but may overfit to AI investment cycles |
| M&A decision support | High value, but narrower and more specialized |
| Board intelligence | Executive-relevant, but difficult as first daily workflow |
| Enterprise risk intelligence | Strong adjacency, but risk-focused rather than decision-system focused |

---

## Decision

Freeze Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support as the initial wedge.

Layer 7 Product Definition should translate this wedge into specific use cases, user stories, and acceptance criteria.

---

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Wedge sounds like consulting workflow | Productize it around repeatable decision objects, lifecycle, and institutional memory |
| Scope expands into all portfolio management | Limit MVP to decision support, not full PPM replacement |
| Adoption is blocked by existing meeting rituals | Position Atlas as upgrading existing governance forums rather than replacing them |

---

## Final Approval

Frozen for Layer 3.
