## Core Customer Thesis

Atlas should not target "the enterprise" as an abstract customer.

Atlas should target organizations with high decision complexity:

- Multiple business units or geographies
- Recurring strategic investment reviews
- Formal executive governance forums
- Cross-functional portfolio or transformation decisions
- High-value initiatives competing for limited budget
- Fragmented evidence, assumptions, and ownership

The first user is not the CEO. The first user is the team preparing executive decisions.

## Key Customer Insight

Customers usually do not say:

> We need a Decision Operating System.

They are more likely to say:

- Our portfolio process is too slow.
- We cannot align on which initiatives deserve funding.
- We keep revisiting the same decisions.
- We do not know why past decisions were made.
- We need better governance over strategic investments.

Product implication:

> Build an Enterprise Decision Operating System, but sell better portfolio governance, faster strategic alignment, smarter capital allocation, and reduced decision risk.

## Customer Validation Caveat

This layer is reconstructed from prior strategic reasoning and internal product critique.

It is not yet based on primary customer interviews.

The assumptions in this layer must be validated later through:

- Enterprise stakeholder interviews
- Strategy and transformation leader feedback
- Prototype review sessions
- GTM messaging tests
- Pilot or simulated workflow evaluation

## Decision

Freeze Layer 4 around a decision-complexity ICP, a strategy/transformation/portfolio champion model, and customer language centered on portfolio governance and decision risk reduction.
