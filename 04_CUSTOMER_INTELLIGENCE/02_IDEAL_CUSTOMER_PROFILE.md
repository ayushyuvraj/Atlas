# Ideal Customer Profile

## Document Status

**Layer:** 4 - Customer Intelligence
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Reconstructed from prior Flagship Project chat.
**Purpose:** Define the organizations most likely to experience the problem Atlas solves.

---

## Segmentation Principle

Atlas should segment customers by:

> Organizational decision complexity

not by:

- Industry alone
- Company size alone
- AI maturity alone
- Geography alone

Decision complexity is the strongest fit because the product is designed for strategic decisions that require cross-functional evidence, assumptions, tradeoffs, and executive alignment.

---

## Primary ICP

Atlas is designed first for organizations that:

- Have 5,000+ employees
- Operate across multiple business units or geographies
- Run formal investment, portfolio, or transformation governance
- Make recurring multi-million-dollar strategic decisions
- Have cross-functional executive committees
- Already use enterprise collaboration and analytics platforms
- Are actively investing in AI, digital transformation, or strategic modernization
- Experience friction in prioritization, capital allocation, transformation governance, or portfolio decisions

---

## Example Organization Types

The ICP can include:

- Global manufacturers
- Retail and consumer goods enterprises
- Healthcare systems
- Technology companies
- Logistics and supply chain organizations
- Telecom operators
- Financial institutions
- Energy companies
- Public sector organizations

These examples are illustrative. The product identity remains horizontal.

---

## Tier 2 Expansion ICP

After the primary ICP, Atlas may expand to organizations that:

- Have 1,000-5,000 employees
- Are scaling rapidly
- Manage multiple product lines or business units
- Are building more formal strategy, portfolio, or transformation governance
- Are beginning to experience decision complexity before full enterprise scale

---

## Non-Customers

Atlas should not initially target:

- Small businesses
- Individual founders
- Freelancers
- Consumer users
- Teams without structured decision governance
- Organizations with fewer than roughly 500 employees
- Organizations seeking only generic document search or meeting summaries

---

## Why Fortune 500 Alone Was Rejected

Fortune 500 is too broad as an ICP.

A mining company, a software platform company, a hospital network, and a retailer may all appear similar by size but have different decision rhythms, governance structures, and adoption paths.

The more useful segment is:

> Large or complex organizations with formal, recurring, cross-functional strategic decision forums.

---

## Why Regulated Industries Alone Were Rejected

Regulated industries are relevant and may produce strong examples, but they should not define the product identity.

If Atlas is framed primarily as a regulated-industry product, it risks narrowing a horizontal enterprise platform into a vertical tool.

---

## ICP Qualification Questions

A strong prospect should answer yes to most of these:

1. Do strategic initiatives compete for limited budget?
2. Does the organization run recurring portfolio, investment, or transformation reviews?
3. Are decisions prepared across several functions?
4. Are assumptions often debated or unclear?
5. Is decision rationale difficult to reconstruct later?
6. Do leaders care about governance, traceability, and alignment?
7. Is there an existing executive forum where Atlas can augment the workflow?

---

## Decision

Freeze the primary ICP as organizations with high organizational decision complexity, especially those with recurring strategic portfolio and investment governance.
