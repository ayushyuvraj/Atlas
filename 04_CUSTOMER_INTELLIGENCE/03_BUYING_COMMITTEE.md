# Buying Committee

## Document Status

**Layer:** 4 - Customer Intelligence
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Reconstructed from prior Flagship Project chat.
**Purpose:** Define the enterprise buying committee, adoption roles, objections, and decision dynamics for Atlas.

---

## Purpose

Atlas is an enterprise platform. It will not be bought, approved, adopted, or renewed by one person.

This document defines the buying committee so later GTM, onboarding, pricing, security, and product requirements can reflect real enterprise adoption dynamics.

---

## Buying Committee Map

| Role | Likely Titles | Primary Concern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economic Buyer | Chief Strategy Officer, Chief Information Officer, Chief Transformation Officer | Will this improve strategic execution and reduce decision risk? |
| Executive Sponsor | CEO, COO, CFO | Will this help leadership make better strategic investments? |
| Champion | Head of Strategy, Director of Enterprise Transformation, Portfolio Director, Product Strategy Lead | Will this help my team prepare better executive decisions with less effort? |
| Power User | Strategy Manager, Strategy Analyst, Portfolio Manager, Transformation Manager, Program Director | Will this reduce manual work and improve traceability? |
| Influencer | Finance, Risk, Product, Operations, Enterprise Architecture, IT Security, HR | Will this fit existing governance, controls, and workflows? |
| Procurement / Legal | Procurement, Vendor Risk, Legal, Compliance | Is this secure, compliant, affordable, and contractually acceptable? |

---

## Economic Buyer

### Likely Titles

- Chief Strategy Officer
- Chief Information Officer
- Chief Transformation Officer

### What They Care About

- Strategic execution quality
- Decision traceability
- Portfolio governance
- AI adoption credibility
- Reduced strategic risk
- Cross-functional alignment

### Objections

- "We already have governance forums."
- "This sounds like another planning tool."
- "Will leaders trust AI-supported recommendations?"
- "How does this integrate with our existing tools?"

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Likely Titles

- CEO
- COO
- CFO

### What They Care About

- Better capital allocation
- Faster leadership alignment
- Reduced rework in executive meetings
- Clear accountability
- Confidence in major decisions

### Product Implication

Executives need concise, boardroom-ready views, not analyst-level configuration.

---

## Champion

### Likely Titles

- Head of Strategy
- Director of Enterprise Transformation
- Portfolio Director
- Product Strategy Lead

### Why This Persona Matters

The champion is the person whose work gets easier and whose credibility improves if Atlas succeeds.

This persona prepares executive decision materials, aligns stakeholders, collects inputs, manages tradeoffs, and often owns the invisible work behind strategic decisions.

### What They Care About

- Faster preparation
- Stronger recommendations
- Better assumption visibility
- Fewer repeated debates
- More credible executive materials
- Clear decision records

---

## Power Users

### Likely Titles

- Strategy Analyst
- Strategy Manager
- Portfolio Manager
- Transformation Manager
- Program Director

### Daily Workflow

Power users:

- Collect documents, data, and stakeholder input
- Reconcile conflicting assumptions
- Prepare executive decks
- Maintain initiative lists
- Track decisions and follow-ups
- Answer "why did we decide this?" questions later

### Product Implication

The product must reduce manual synthesis work while improving traceability and decision quality.

## Influencers

| Influencer | Concern |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ROI, budget, capital allocation, financial assumptions |
| Risk | Governance, auditability, decision risk, controls |
| Product Leadership | Portfolio tradeoffs, roadmap impact, customer outcomes |
| Operations | Execution feasibility and dependencies |
| Enterprise Architecture | Integration fit and platform architecture |
| IT Security | Access control, data handling, vendor risk |
| HR / Change Management | Adoption and behavior change |

---

## Procurement and Vendor Risk

Procurement will evaluate:

- Security
- Compliance
- Data access
- Integration requirements
- Vendor reliability
- Pricing
- Contract terms

This means later layers must include a credible security overview, deployment model, and procurement-ready value narrative.

---

## Decision

Freeze the buying committee model with the champion as the primary adoption focus, the strategy/transformation/CIO layer as economic buyers, and the executive team as sponsor and beneficiary.
