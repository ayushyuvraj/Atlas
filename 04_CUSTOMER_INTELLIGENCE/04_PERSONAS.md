## Persona Summary

| Persona | Role in Adoption | Product Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy Director | Primary champion | Owns decision preparation and executive recommendations |
| Transformation Leader | Champion / power user | Uses Atlas for transformation governance and initiative decisions |
| Portfolio Manager | Power user | Manages initiative comparison, prioritization, and tracking |
| Chief Strategy Officer | Economic buyer / executive sponsor | Owns enterprise strategy and decision quality |
| Executive Leadership Team | Beneficiary / decision maker | Reviews recommendations and makes final decisions |

---

## Persona 1 - Strategy Director

### Role

Primary champion.

### Responsibilities

- Lead enterprise strategic planning
- Prepare executive recommendations
- Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders
- Own portfolio prioritization inputs
- Translate analysis into executive-ready narratives

### Pain Points

- Weeks spent preparing executive reviews
- Conflicting stakeholder inputs
- Weak traceability across decks, spreadsheets, and meetings
- Repeated debates on similar topics
- Lost decision rationale after meetings

### Desired Outcomes

- Faster executive alignment
- More credible recommendations
- Higher confidence in assumptions
- Reduced preparation effort
- Better institutional memory

### Atlas Value

Atlas helps the Strategy Director turn fragmented evidence and stakeholder input into a structured decision workspace and executive-ready recommendation.

---

## Persona 2 - Transformation Leader

### Role

Champion or power user.

### Responsibilities

- Oversee strategic transformation programs
- Track initiative health
- Escalate execution risks
- Manage cross-functional dependencies
- Support governance forums

### Pain Points

- Fragmented reporting
- Limited visibility into dependencies
- Difficult prioritization
- Poor linkage between strategic decisions and execution outcomes
- Repeated surprises during governance reviews

### Desired Outcomes

- Improved governance
- Better resource allocation
- Earlier risk visibility
- Clearer decision-to-execution traceability

### Atlas Value

Atlas helps the Transformation Leader connect decision rationale, initiative status, risk, and outcome learning.

---

## Persona 3 - Portfolio Manager

### Role

Power user.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain initiative portfolio
- Compare investments and tradeoffs
- Prepare prioritization materials
- Track decisions and follow-ups
- Coordinate inputs across finance, product, technology, and operations

### Pain Points

- Too many initiatives competing for limited budget
- Inconsistent scoring across teams
- Manual deck and spreadsheet reconciliation
- Unclear why initiatives were approved, paused, or killed

### Desired Outcomes

- Clear prioritization logic
- Better comparison of tradeoffs
- Faster preparation
- Reusable decision records

### Atlas Value

Atlas gives the Portfolio Manager a structured way to compare initiatives and preserve the reasoning behind funding decisions.

---

## Persona 4 - Chief Strategy Officer

### Role

Economic buyer and executive sponsor.

### Responsibilities

- Own enterprise strategy
- Advise executive leadership
- Oversee strategic investment decisions
- Align business units around strategic priorities
- Improve governance quality

### Pain Points

- Executive disagreement
- Poor assumption visibility
- Limited institutional learning
- Difficulty proving why decisions were made
- Slow alignment across functions

### Desired Outcomes

- Better executive decisions
- Greater organizational alignment
- Higher confidence in strategic investments
- More rigorous portfolio governance

### Atlas Value

Atlas gives the Chief Strategy Officer a system for making strategic reasoning explicit, reviewable, and reusable.

---

## Persona 5 - Executive Leadership Team

### Role

Final decision makers and ultimate beneficiaries.

### Responsibilities

- Approve, delay, reject, or fund strategic initiatives
- Allocate resources
- Accept accountability for enterprise direction
- Resolve tradeoffs across functions

### Needs

- Concise executive summaries
- Transparent reasoning
- Comparable scenarios
- Clear risks and assumptions
- Actionable recommendations
- Confidence that disagreement has been surfaced

### Product Relationship

Executives are not the primary operators of Atlas.

They need a clear, focused, inspectable decision review experience.

---

## Persona Design Implications

- Design for daily users who prepare decisions.
- Make executive outputs concise and inspectable.
- Avoid forcing executives into configuration-heavy workflows.
- Give analysts depth without cluttering executive views.
- Make traceability valuable to champions and governance teams.

Freeze these personas as the Layer 4 persona model.
