## Core Customer Problem

Large organizations make high-stakes strategic decisions through fragmented preparation and informal reasoning.

The problem is not that organizations lack information.

The problem is that evidence, assumptions, stakeholder perspectives, rationale, and outcomes are rarely assembled into a reusable decision system.

---

## Functional Jobs

When preparing strategic portfolio and investment decisions, customers need to:

- Frame the decision clearly
- Collect relevant evidence
- Reconcile conflicting inputs
- Surface hidden assumptions
- Evaluate multiple scenarios
- Compare strategic alternatives
- Align stakeholders
- Prepare executive recommendations
- Document final rationale
- Preserve institutional knowledge
- Learn from previous decisions

---

## Emotional Jobs

Customers want to feel:

- Confident
- Prepared
- Credible
- In control
- Less exposed in executive forums
- Clear about what evidence supports the recommendation

---

## Social Jobs

Customers want to be recognized for:

- Making better strategic recommendations
- Improving executive decision quality
- Leading stronger transformation or portfolio governance
- Building trust across functions
- Bringing rigor to ambiguous decisions

## Pain Themes

| Pain | What Happens Today | Product Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow preparation | Teams spend weeks gathering slides, spreadsheets, and comments | Atlas must reduce decision-prep friction |
| Conflicting assumptions | Functions use different numbers and narratives | Atlas must make assumptions explicit and comparable |
| Weak traceability | Rationale disappears after the meeting | Atlas must create durable decision records |
| Repeated debates | Similar questions keep returning | Atlas must preserve decision memory and prior reasoning |
| Poor scenario discipline | Alternatives are discussed inconsistently | Atlas must support scenario comparison |
| Limited accountability | Owners, review dates, and outcomes are unclear | Atlas must link decisions to owners, metrics, and outcome review |

---

## Current Alternatives

| Tool / Process | Why Customers Use It | Why It Falls Short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PowerPoint | Executive communication | Static and weak for reasoning traceability |
| Excel | Financial analysis and comparison | No institutional reasoning or decision memory |
| Teams / Email | Collaboration | Context and rationale fragment quickly |
| BI dashboards | Historical performance visibility | Does not guide future strategic choice |
| Notion / Confluence / SharePoint | Knowledge storage | Stores information but does not challenge assumptions |
| ChatGPT or generic AI tools | Fast synthesis and drafting | Lacks governed institutional memory and decision lifecycle |
| PPM tools | Portfolio tracking | Often tracks execution more than reasoning quality |

---

## High-Value Job Statement

When a strategic portfolio or investment review is approaching, help strategy and portfolio teams prepare an evidence-backed recommendation that surfaces assumptions, compares tradeoffs, aligns stakeholders, and preserves the final rationale for future learning.
