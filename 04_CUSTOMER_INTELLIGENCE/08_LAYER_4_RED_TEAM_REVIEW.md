## Red Team Perspectives

### Product Leadership

**Challenge:** The customer model may still be too broad because many enterprises have decision complexity.

**Response:** Layer 4 narrows the initial target to organizations with recurring strategic portfolio, investment, or transformation governance forums.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Enterprise Buyer

**Challenge:** Buyers may not describe their problem as a "decision" problem.

**Response:** Layer 4 translates product language into customer language: portfolio governance, strategic alignment, capital allocation, and reduced decision risk.

**Status:** Addressed; requires validation.

---

### Champion

**Challenge:** The strategy could overfocus on executives and ignore the teams doing the work.

**Response:** Layer 4 identifies strategy, transformation, and portfolio leaders as champions and power users.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Daily User

**Challenge:** If Atlas adds more process, analysts may reject it.

**Response:** Layer 4 frames user value around reducing manual synthesis, improving traceability, and producing stronger executive recommendations.

**Status:** Addressed; must be validated in UX.

---

### Enterprise Sales

**Challenge:** The buyer path may be ambiguous because several executives could own the budget.

**Response:** Layer 4 documents multiple likely economic buyers and carries forward an open question about the strongest first outbound target.

**Status:** Accepted with open question.

---

### Procurement and Security

**Challenge:** Strategic decision data is sensitive, and procurement may slow adoption.

**Response:** Layer 4 logs procurement and security concerns for Technical Architecture, Security Overview, and GTM work.

**Status:** Accepted as later-layer requirement.

---

### GTM

**Challenge:** Category language may be premature.

**Response:** Layer 4 recommends leading with customer workflows and business outcomes before broader EDOS language.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Skeptical Critic

**Challenge:** The customer intelligence is reconstructed and not yet based on interviews.

**Response:** Every Layer 4 artifact is labeled as reconstructed from prior working decisions, and validation is preserved as an open risk.

**Status:** Accepted with validation caveat.
