## Definition

Institutional Reasoning Management is the discipline of making enterprise decision-making explicit, structured, reviewable, and reusable.

It treats reasoning as an organizational capability rather than an individual act.

---

## Why It Exists

Enterprises often preserve:

- Documents
- Dashboards
- Meeting notes
- Approvals
- Project plans

They less consistently preserve:

- Why decisions were made
- Which assumptions mattered
- Which alternatives were rejected
- Which disagreement was unresolved
- Which outcomes proved assumptions right or wrong
- What the organization should learn next time

Institutional Reasoning Management closes that gap.

---

## The IRM Loop

Atlas operationalizes Institutional Reasoning Management through the loop:

```text
Sense -> Challenge -> Simulate -> Decide -> Execute -> Learn
```

---

## Stage 1 - Sense

Build situational awareness.

The product gathers and organizes:

- Context
- Evidence
- Strategic objectives
- Constraints
- Dependencies
- Prior decisions
- Stakeholder inputs

Outcome:

> The organization understands the decision environment.

---

## Stage 2 - Challenge

Expose what may be wrong, weak, missing, or biased.

The product surfaces:

- Hidden assumptions
- Conflicting evidence
- Missing stakeholders
- Weak signals
- Overconfidence
- Blind spots

Outcome:

> The organization improves the quality of debate before reaching a recommendation.

---

## Stage 3 - Simulate

Explore plausible futures and compare alternatives.

The product evaluates:

- Scenarios
- Tradeoffs
- Risks
- Financial impact
- Operational feasibility
- Strategic fit
- Second-order effects

Outcome:

> The organization understands consequences before commitment.

---

## Stage 4 - Decide

Generate an explainable recommendation and capture the final human decision.

The product supports:

- Recommendation synthesis
- Confidence levels
- Alternatives considered
- Decision owner
- Review date
- Success metrics
- Rationale capture

Outcome:

> The organization makes a decision with traceable reasoning.

---

## Stage 5 - Execute

Connect the decision to follow-through.

The product tracks:

- Owners
- Milestones
- Dependencies
- Risks
- Escalations
- Changes

Outcome:

> The decision remains connected to execution reality.

---

## Stage 6 - Learn

Compare expected outcomes with actual outcomes.

The product captures:

- Which assumptions held
- Which assumptions failed
- What surprised the organization
- Which patterns should influence future decisions
- Which templates or reasoning paths should improve

Outcome:

> The organization becomes better at future decisions.

---

## Decision

Freeze the IRM Loop as the canonical decision methodology behind Atlas.

Future lifecycle, UX, AI behavior, analytics, and technical design should map back to one or more stages of this loop.
