# Institutional Reasoning Engine

## Document Status

**Layer:** 5 - Decision Science
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Reconstructed from prior Flagship Project chat and Layer 3 core differentiator.
**Purpose:** Define the Institutional Reasoning Engine as the product system that implements Institutional Reasoning Management.

---

## Definition

The Institutional Reasoning Engine is the Atlas product engine that structures, challenges, synthesizes, and preserves reasoning for strategic enterprise decisions.

It is not a single model, chatbot, or prompt.

It is a product system that coordinates reasoning modules, decision context, institutional memory, and human review.

---

## Role In Atlas

The IRE powers the Decision Workspace by helping users:

- Frame a strategic decision
- Assemble relevant context
- Surface assumptions
- Evaluate alternatives
- Compare scenarios
- Explain tradeoffs
- Generate recommendations
- Capture decision rationale
- Learn from outcomes

---

## Inputs

The IRE may use:

- Decision statement
- Business objective
- Constraints
- Stakeholders
- Evidence
- Prior decisions
- Initiative data
- Market context
- Risk data
- User-provided assumptions
- Enterprise policy or governance context

---

## Outputs

The IRE produces:

- Context brief
- Assumption map
- Evidence summary
- Perspective analysis
- Scenario set
- Tradeoff analysis
- Risk assessment
- Executive recommendation
- Confidence rationale
- Decision record inputs
- Learning prompts

---

## Operating Rules

1. The IRE recommends, but does not decide.
2. Every recommendation must expose evidence and assumptions.
3. Confidence must be represented as a judgment with rationale, not as false precision.
4. Disagreement should be visible, not hidden.
5. Missing evidence should be stated clearly.
6. Historical reasoning should be reused only when relevant.
7. The system must preserve enough traceability for review and audit.

---

## Why This Is Different From Generic AI

Generic AI often optimizes for answer generation.

The IRE optimizes for decision reasoning.

The distinction:

| Generic AI | Institutional Reasoning Engine |
| --- | --- |
| Answers a question | Runs a structured decision process |
| Summarizes information | Connects evidence, assumptions, and tradeoffs |
| Produces text | Produces reusable decision artifacts |
| May hide uncertainty | Makes uncertainty inspectable |
| Often stateless | Builds institutional memory over time |
| User asks, AI answers | User and AI jointly reason toward human decision |

---

## Core Hypothesis

The IRE becomes more valuable as it accumulates decision records, assumption history, outcome reviews, and reusable patterns.

This is a strategic hypothesis and must be tested during later validation and build phases.

---

## Decision

Freeze IRE v1.0 as a structured product engine composed of reasoning modules, institutional memory, and human review rather than a single visible AI assistant.
