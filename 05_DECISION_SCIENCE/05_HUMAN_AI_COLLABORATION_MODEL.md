# Human-AI Collaboration Model

## Document Status

**Layer:** 5 - Decision Science
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Reconstructed from prior Flagship Project chat and product philosophy.
**Purpose:** Define the boundaries between AI reasoning and human accountability.

---

## Core Principle

AI reasons. Humans decide.

Atlas should improve human judgment, not replace it.

---

## Collaboration Roles

| Activity | AI Role | Human Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame decision | Ask clarifying questions and suggest structure | Confirm decision statement and scope |
| Gather context | Summarize relevant evidence and prior decisions | Validate relevance and fill gaps |
| Surface assumptions | Identify explicit and hidden assumptions | Accept, reject, edit, or add assumptions |
| Analyze evidence | Compare sources and summarize implications | Judge source quality and business relevance |
| Generate scenarios | Draft plausible alternatives and consequences | Select scenarios worth reviewing |
| Assess risk | Highlight risk categories and uncertainty | Apply judgment and accountability |
| Recommend | Produce explainable recommendation options | Decide whether to accept, modify, or reject |
| Commit | Prepare decision record | Approve decision and own outcome |
| Learn | Compare assumptions to outcomes | Validate lessons and adjust future practice |

---

## AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

- Ask clarifying questions
- Organize evidence
- Surface assumptions
- Show disagreement
- Compare alternatives
- Explain tradeoffs
- State uncertainty
- Recommend next actions
- Preserve reasoning artifacts

---

## Human Responsibilities

Humans must:

- Define what decision is being made
- Provide business judgment
- Validate evidence relevance
- Challenge recommendations
- Accept accountability
- Approve final decisions
- Review outcomes
- Decide what lessons become institutional knowledge

---

## Boundaries

The AI must not:

- Approve investments
- Cancel initiatives
- Assign final accountability
- Hide uncertainty
- Invent evidence
- Present recommendations as mandates
- Override human decision rights

---

## Collaboration Modes

### Executive Mode

Concise, recommendation-first, inspectable.

Designed for leaders who need clear reasoning and decision options quickly.

### Analyst Mode

Detailed, evidence-rich, traceable.

Designed for teams preparing decision materials and validating assumptions.

### Governance Mode

Audit-oriented.

Designed for decision records, approvals, review dates, and outcome learning.

---

## Decision

Freeze human accountability as a non-negotiable product principle.

Every future AI behavior, UX flow, and approval model must preserve human decision rights.
