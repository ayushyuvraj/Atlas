## Product Summary

Atlas is an Enterprise Decision Operating System for strategic portfolio and investment decisions.

The MVP provides a Decision Workspace where strategy, transformation, and portfolio teams can frame a high-stakes decision, assemble context, run structured institutional reasoning, review AI-assisted recommendations, and capture a reusable Decision Record.

Atlas does not replace executive judgment. It makes the reasoning behind strategic decisions more explicit, transparent, and reusable.

---

## Problem Statement

Enterprise strategic decisions are often prepared across disconnected documents, meetings, spreadsheets, dashboards, and informal conversations.

This creates recurring problems:

- Decision context is scattered.
- Assumptions remain implicit.
- Stakeholder perspectives are not evaluated consistently.
- Recommendations are difficult to trace back to evidence.
- Final decisions lose their rationale after meetings.
- Organizations struggle to learn from prior strategic decisions.

The core product problem is the absence of a structured system for institutional reasoning.

---

## MVP Wedge

The MVP focuses on:

**Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support**

Initial decision examples:

- Which initiatives should receive funding?
- Which transformation programs should be prioritized?
- Which product or market investments should be advanced, paused, or rejected?
- Which scarce resources should be allocated to competing strategic options?

## Primary Users

| User Group | Product Role |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy Directors | Own and coordinate decision preparation |
| Portfolio Managers | Compare initiatives and track recommendation quality |
| Transformation Leaders | Evaluate strategic programs and dependencies |
| Executive Sponsors | Review recommendations and make final decisions |
| Analysts | Add evidence, assumptions, and scenario inputs |

---

## Product Goals

1. Create a structured workspace for strategic decisions.
2. Convert scattered inputs into a coherent Context Brief.
3. Surface assumptions, risks, tradeoffs, and alternatives.
4. Generate explainable executive recommendations.
5. Capture final decision rationale as a reusable Decision Record.
6. Create the foundation for institutional decision memory.

---

## MVP Scope

### In Scope

- Decision creation
- Decision templates
- Context assembly
- Evidence entry and summarization
- Assumption mapping
- Scenario comparison
- Risk and tradeoff analysis
- AI-assisted recommendation generation
- Human review and challenge
- Decision commitment
- Decision Record
- Basic decision history and reuse

### Out of Scope

- Full project portfolio management replacement
- Full live meeting assistant
- Autonomous approval
- Real-time enterprise-wide integrations
- Production-grade workflow automation
- Cross-company benchmarking
- Fully automated execution tracking

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Product Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Decision cycle time | Time from Decision creation to commitment |
| Evidence completeness | Percentage of required evidence fields completed |
| Assumption coverage | Number and quality of explicit assumptions |
| Scenario coverage | Number of alternatives compared |
| Recommendation traceability | Percentage of recommendations linked to evidence and assumptions |
| Decision reuse | Prior decisions referenced in new decisions |
| Review quality | Number of meaningful challenges or additions before commitment |
