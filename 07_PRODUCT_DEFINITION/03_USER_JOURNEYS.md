# User Journeys

## Document Status

**Layer:** 7 - Product Definition
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layer 4 personas and Layer 6 Decision Lifecycle.
**Purpose:** Define the user journeys that the MVP must support.

---

## Journey 1 - Strategy Director Prepares a Decision

### Starting Point

A strategic investment review is approaching and multiple initiatives are competing for funding.

### Journey

1. Creates a new Decision Workspace.
2. Selects the Strategic Investment template.
3. Defines the decision statement, objective, deadline, options, and decision owner.
4. Invites analysts and stakeholders to contribute evidence.
5. Reviews the Context Brief.
6. Runs institutional reasoning.
7. Reviews assumptions, tradeoffs, risks, scenarios, and recommendation.
8. Challenges weak assumptions or requests additional evidence.
9. Finalizes an executive recommendation.
10. Presents the recommendation to the executive sponsor.

### Desired Outcome

The Strategy Director enters the decision forum with a structured recommendation instead of a fragmented deck.

---

## Journey 2 - Analyst Builds the Context Brief

### Starting Point

The Decision Workspace exists, but the context is incomplete.

### Journey

1. Opens assigned Decision Workspace.
2. Adds business background, documents, KPIs, dependencies, and evidence.
3. Reviews AI-generated evidence summaries.
4. Tags evidence by type and confidence.
5. Adds known assumptions and constraints.
6. Submits the Context Brief for review.

### Desired Outcome

The decision team has shared situational awareness before reasoning begins.

---

## Journey 3 - Portfolio Manager Compares Options

### Starting Point

Multiple initiatives are competing for limited budget or resources.

### Journey

1. Opens the Scenario Comparison view.
2. Reviews each option across financial, strategic, operational, and risk dimensions.
3. Inspects tradeoffs and second-order effects.
4. Flags incomplete or weak evidence.
5. Adds comments on feasibility and dependencies.
6. Supports or challenges the recommendation.

### Desired Outcome

Options are compared consistently rather than debated through isolated stakeholder preferences.

---

## Journey 4 - Executive Sponsor Reviews Recommendation

### Starting Point

The Strategy Director has generated an executive recommendation.

### Journey

1. Opens the Executive Recommendation.
2. Reads concise summary and recommended option.
3. Expands evidence, assumptions, risks, and alternatives where needed.
4. Reviews confidence and open questions.
5. Requests revision or commits the decision.
6. Confirms owner, timeline, success metrics, and review date.

### Desired Outcome

The executive sponsor can make or sponsor a decision with transparent rationale.

---

## Journey 5 - Team Reuses Prior Decision Memory

### Starting Point

A new decision resembles a prior strategic decision.

### Journey

1. Creates or opens a Decision Workspace.
2. Reviews related prior decisions surfaced by Atlas.
3. Inspects previous assumptions, outcomes, and lessons.
4. Applies relevant learning to the new decision.
5. Updates the new recommendation with historical context.

### Desired Outcome

The organization avoids repeating avoidable mistakes and uses prior reasoning as an asset.

---

## Decision

Freeze these journeys as the MVP journey baseline for PRD, UX, and technical architecture.
