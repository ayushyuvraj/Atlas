# Journey Maps

## Document Status

**Layer:** 8 - Experience Design
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layer 7 user journeys and Layer 4 personas.
**Purpose:** Translate product journeys into experience maps with user goals, touchpoints, pain points, and design implications.

---

## Journey 1 - Strategy Director Prepares a Decision

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Design Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Create a structured decision | New Decision Wizard | Setup can feel heavy | Use template-first creation with only essential fields |
| Frame | Clarify decision scope | Decision Framing panel | Ambiguous decisions create weak outputs | Show required framing prompts and scope boundaries |
| Assemble | Bring context together | Context Brief | Evidence is scattered | Provide guided evidence sections and completeness indicators |
| Reason | Generate structured analysis | Reasoning Canvas | AI output may feel opaque | Show assumptions, scenarios, tradeoffs, risks, and confidence separately |
| Review | Prepare executive recommendation | Executive Recommendation panel | Too much detail for executives | Provide concise summary with expandable evidence |
| Commit | Record final decision | Decision Record | Rationale often disappears | Require owner, success metrics, assumptions, risks, and review date |

## Journey 2 - Analyst Builds Context

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Design Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Receive | Understand assigned decision | Decision Workspace overview | Context may be unclear | Show decision objective and missing sections |
| Add | Enter supporting information | Evidence Explorer | Evidence quality varies | Capture source, type, owner, summary, and confidence |
| Summarize | Create shared context | Context Brief | Summaries may be too long | Use structured summaries by evidence category |
| Submit | Mark context ready | Context completion state | Team may not know readiness | Show readiness indicator and missing critical inputs |

## Journey 3 - Portfolio Manager Compares Options

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Design Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Review | Compare alternatives | Scenario Comparison | Options are debated inconsistently | Use consistent comparison columns |
| Evaluate | Understand tradeoffs | Tradeoff Matrix | Opportunity costs are hidden | Show benefit, cost, opportunity cost, reversibility, and second-order effects |
| Challenge | Flag weak reasoning | Challenge controls | Weak assumptions survive meetings | Allow comments and challenges on assumptions, evidence, and recommendation |
| Align | Support recommendation | Decision Review | Stakeholder disagreement is unclear | Summarize agreements, disagreements, and open questions |

---

## Journey 4 - Executive Sponsor Reviews Recommendation

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Design Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scan | Understand recommendation fast | Executive Summary | Too much detail slows review | Lead with recommendation, confidence, risks, and open questions |
| Inspect | Drill into rationale | Expandable sections | Opaque AI undermines trust | Show evidence, assumptions, alternatives, and tradeoffs on demand |
| Decide | Commit or request revision | Decision Commitment panel | Accountability can be unclear | Require final decision, owner, metrics, accepted risks, and review date |

---

## Journey 5 - Team Reuses Decision Memory

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint | Pain Point | Design Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Find relevant prior decisions | Institutional Memory panel | Prior decisions are hard to locate | Surface related decisions by type, objective, assumptions, and outcomes |
| Compare | Understand relevance | Prior Decision card | Prior context may not apply | Explain why the prior decision is relevant and how it differs |
| Apply | Use learning in current decision | Context and assumption panels | Lessons remain generic | Link prior assumptions and outcomes to current reasoning |

## Decision

Freeze these journey maps as the Layer 8 experience baseline.
