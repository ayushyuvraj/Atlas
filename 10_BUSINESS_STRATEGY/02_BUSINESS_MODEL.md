# Business Model

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from ICP, buying committee, and MVP wedge.
**Purpose:** Define how Atlas can become a commercially viable enterprise product.

---

## Business Model Recommendation

Atlas should use an enterprise SaaS subscription model with annual contracts.

The model should combine:

- Platform subscription
- Workspace or decision volume limits
- Seat-based access for power users
- Enterprise add-ons for governance, integrations, and advanced memory

---

## Buyer Logic

Atlas should be sold to organizations where decision complexity creates measurable cost, delay, and risk.

The economic buyer is likely:

- Chief Strategy Officer
- Chief Transformation Officer
- Chief Information Officer

Executive sponsors may include:

- CEO
- COO
- CFO

---

## Value Drivers

| Value Driver | Business Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Faster decision preparation | Reduced weeks spent on fragmented analysis |
| Better alignment | Fewer unresolved executive disagreements |
| Stronger investment prioritization | Better use of scarce budget and resources |
| Decision traceability | Clear rationale for major commitments |
| Reduced rework | Fewer repeated debates and duplicated analysis |
| Institutional learning | Prior decisions improve future decisions |

---

## Revenue Model

### Primary Revenue

Annual enterprise subscription.

### Expansion Revenue

- Additional workspaces
- Additional decision templates
- Advanced institutional memory
- Enterprise integrations
- Governance and audit add-ons
- Premium support and implementation

---

## Services Boundary

Atlas may require implementation support, but the business should remain software-led.

Services should support:

- Initial decision-template configuration
- Pilot onboarding
- Workflow mapping
- Training
- Success measurement

Services should not become the primary business model.

---

## Decision

Freeze annual enterprise SaaS subscription as the primary business model hypothesis.
