# Pricing and Packaging

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Business hypothesis derived from ICP, value drivers, and enterprise SaaS patterns.
**Purpose:** Define a testable pricing and packaging hypothesis for Atlas.

---

## Pricing Principle

Atlas should price around enterprise decision value, not individual AI usage.

The product supports high-stakes strategic decisions where even small improvements in speed, alignment, or risk reduction may justify enterprise-level pricing.

---

## Packaging Hypothesis

### Package 1 - Team

For one strategy, transformation, or portfolio team.

Includes:

- Limited Decision Workspaces
- Strategic Investment template
- Core reasoning modules
- Decision Records
- Basic analytics
- Standard support

---

### Package 2 - Business Unit

For a business unit or transformation office.

Includes:

- More Decision Workspaces
- Multiple decision templates
- Institutional Memory
- Advanced analytics
- Role-based governance
- Implementation support

---

### Package 3 - Enterprise

For enterprise-wide decision governance.

Includes:

- Multiple business units
- Advanced governance
- Enterprise integrations
- Security and audit controls
- Custom templates
- Premium support
- Executive reporting

---

## Pricing Metrics

Potential pricing metrics:

- Number of Decision Workspaces
- Number of power users
- Number of business units
- Advanced governance features
- Integration tier
- Institutional Memory capacity

Recommended primary metric:

**Decision Workspaces plus enterprise tier**

Rationale:

- Better aligned with value than simple seat count.
- Avoids punishing broad executive visibility.
- Ties pricing to decision complexity.

---

## Pricing Validation Questions

- Does the buyer perceive decision quality as budget-worthy?
- Is pricing easier to justify by decision volume, team size, or portfolio value?
- Should executive viewers be free or low-cost?
- Should AI usage be included or separately metered?
- What budget category would this purchase come from?

---

## Decision

Freeze a tiered enterprise SaaS packaging hypothesis.

Exact price points remain unvalidated and should be tested during validation.
