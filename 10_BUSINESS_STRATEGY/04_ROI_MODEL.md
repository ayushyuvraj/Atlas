# ROI Model

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0

**Purpose:** Define how Atlas can justify enterprise value.

---

## ROI Thesis

Atlas creates value by reducing the cost, time, and risk of strategic decision-making.

The ROI story should focus on measurable improvements in:

- Decision preparation time
- Executive alignment
- Rework reduction
- Investment prioritization
- Decision traceability
- Risk visibility

---

## ROI Categories

### 1. Time Savings

Current state:

- Strategy teams spend significant time collecting inputs, reconciling assumptions, and preparing decision materials.

Atlas impact:

- Faster context assembly
- Faster recommendation preparation
- Reduced repeated analysis

Measurement:

- Hours saved per decision
- Days from initiation to recommendation

---

### 2. Better Capital Allocation

Current state:

- Investment decisions may be made with inconsistent assumptions and incomplete tradeoff visibility.

Atlas impact:

- More explicit scenario comparison
- Clearer risk-adjusted recommendation
- Better prioritization of competing initiatives

Measurement:

- Portfolio decisions completed with full scenario coverage
- Percentage of decisions linked to success metrics

---

### 3. Reduced Rework

Current state:

- Executive decisions are delayed when assumptions, evidence, or stakeholder inputs are incomplete.

Atlas impact:

- Earlier assumption surfacing
- More transparent tradeoffs
- Better preparation before review forums

Measurement:

- Number of review cycles per decision
- Number of decisions returned for more analysis

---

### 4. Stronger Governance and Traceability

Current state:

- Decision rationale is often lost after approval.

Atlas impact:

- Committed Decision Records
- Audit trail
- Linked evidence and assumptions

Measurement:

- Percentage of committed decisions with complete rationale
- Decision Records reused in future decisions

---

## ROI Formula

Illustrative formula:

```text
Estimated Annual Value =
  Time Savings
+ Reduced Rework
+ Improved Portfolio Allocation
+ Reduced Decision Risk
+ Governance and Audit Value
- Annual Atlas Cost
```

This formula is directional and must be validated with customer data.
