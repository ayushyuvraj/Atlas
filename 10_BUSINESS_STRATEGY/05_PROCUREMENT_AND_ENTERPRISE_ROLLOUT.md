# Procurement and Enterprise Rollout

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layer 4 buying committee and enterprise adoption journey.
**Purpose:** Define how Atlas can enter and expand inside an enterprise.

---

## Procurement Thesis

Atlas should enter through a focused pilot tied to an existing strategic decision forum rather than an enterprise-wide deployment.

The buyer should not be asked to transform the full decision operating model on day one.

---

## Entry Motion

Recommended entry point:

**Strategic Investment Committee Pilot**

Pilot scope:

- One strategy, portfolio, or transformation team
- One decision forum
- One decision template
- A limited number of active Decision Workspaces
- Clear success metrics

---

## Procurement Path

1. Identify business champion.
2. Validate decision workflow pain.
3. Align with executive sponsor.
4. Define pilot use case.
5. Confirm security and data boundaries.
6. Run pilot.
7. Measure impact.
8. Expand to adjacent decision forums.

---

## Buying Committee Concerns

| Stakeholder | Concern | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Strategy Officer | Better decisions and alignment | Portfolio governance and traceability |
| CIO | Security, integrations, AI governance | Role-based access, audit events, model-agnostic design |
| CFO | ROI and cost justification | Time savings, reduced rework, better capital allocation |
| Strategy Director | Preparation burden | Guided workspace and structured recommendation |
| Procurement | Vendor risk and cost | Pilot-first deployment and clear scope |
| Security | Confidential decision data | Access control and audit trail |

---

## Rollout Stages

### Stage 1 - Pilot

Prove value in one decision forum.

### Stage 2 - Department Expansion

Expand to strategy, transformation, or portfolio teams.

### Stage 3 - Business Unit Rollout

Apply additional decision templates.

### Stage 4 - Enterprise Rollout

Create shared institutional decision memory across functions.

---

## Decision

Freeze pilot-led enterprise rollout as the go-to-market implementation path.
