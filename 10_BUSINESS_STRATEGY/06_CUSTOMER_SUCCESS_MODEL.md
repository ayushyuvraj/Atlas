# Customer Success Model

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from adoption journey, product requirements, and ROI model.
**Purpose:** Define how customers become successful with Atlas after purchase or pilot start.

---

## Customer Success Thesis

Atlas adoption succeeds when it improves an existing executive decision ritual rather than forcing a new process from scratch.

Customer success should focus on:

- Pilot setup
- Decision-template configuration
- Workflow adoption
- Outcome measurement
- Expansion readiness

---

## Onboarding Model

### Step 1 - Decision Forum Selection

Choose the first governance forum:

- Investment committee
- Portfolio review
- Transformation review
- Product portfolio review

---

### Step 2 - Template Configuration

Configure the decision template:

- Required context fields
- Evaluation dimensions
- Risk categories
- Approval rules
- Success metrics

---

### Step 3 - First Decision Workspace

Create and run the first live or representative decision.

---

### Step 4 - Review and Improvement

Review:

- Preparation time
- Evidence completeness
- Assumption quality
- Executive feedback
- Decision Record quality

---

### Step 5 - Expansion Plan

Identify next decision forum or team.

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first Decision Workspace | Speed of activation |
| First Decision Record completed | Pilot completion |
| Evidence completeness | Decision quality input |
| Recommendation review rate | Human engagement |
| Executive feedback | Value perception |
| Reuse of prior decision | Institutional memory value |

---

## Customer Success Risks

- Users treat Atlas as extra documentation work.
- Executives only want the summary and ignore reasoning detail.
- Teams do not commit Decision Records.
- No owner is accountable for adoption.
- Pilot is not tied to a real decision forum.

---

## Decision

Freeze this customer success model as the initial adoption and expansion framework.
