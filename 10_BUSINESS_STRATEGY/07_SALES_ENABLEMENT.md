## Sales Narrative

Atlas helps enterprises run strategic portfolio and investment decisions with structured institutional reasoning.

Instead of preparing decisions across disconnected slides, spreadsheets, dashboards, and meetings, teams use a Decision Workspace to assemble context, surface assumptions, compare scenarios, generate explainable recommendations, and preserve final rationale.

---

## Target Buyer Message

### Chief Strategy Officer

Atlas improves portfolio governance, strategic alignment, and decision traceability.

### Chief Transformation Officer

Atlas helps prioritize transformation investments and make dependencies, risks, and assumptions visible.

### Chief Information Officer

Atlas provides a structured, governed AI decision-support layer that can evolve into enterprise decision infrastructure.

### CFO

Atlas helps reduce rework, clarify investment tradeoffs, and improve capital allocation discipline.

---

## Discovery Questions

- How are strategic investment decisions prepared today?
- How long does it take to prepare an executive recommendation?
- Where do assumptions usually live?
- How are alternatives compared?
- What happens when executives disagree?
- How is final decision rationale preserved?
- How often do teams revisit prior decisions?
- What causes investment decisions to be delayed or reopened?

---

## Common Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We already have BI dashboards | Dashboards show what happened; Atlas structures what should be decided next |
| We already use collaboration tools | Collaboration tools store discussion; Atlas preserves decision reasoning |
| We can use generic AI tools | Generic AI lacks decision lifecycle, governance, and institutional memory |
| This sounds like extra process | Atlas upgrades existing decision forums rather than replacing them |
| We need integrations first | Pilot can start with focused inputs before broad integration work |

## Demo Narrative

Recommended demo:

1. Show an active strategic investment decision.
2. Show context and evidence assembled in one workspace.
3. Show assumptions surfaced by AI and reviewed by humans.
4. Compare scenarios and tradeoffs.
5. Generate executive recommendation.
6. Challenge one weak assumption.
7. Commit final Decision Record.
8. Show how the decision becomes reusable memory.
