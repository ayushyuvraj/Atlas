# Expansion Strategy

## Document Status

**Layer:** 10 - Business Strategy
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layer 3 product wedge, Layer 4 ICP, and Layer 15 platform expansion backlog.
**Purpose:** Define how Atlas can expand beyond the first wedge.

---

## Expansion Thesis

Atlas should start with Strategic Portfolio and Investment Decision Support, then expand into adjacent decision workflows that reuse the same Institutional Reasoning Engine and Decision object model.

The platform expansion should not create unrelated products. Every wedge should strengthen the same Decision Operating System.

---

## Expansion Path

```text
Strategic Investment Decisions
        ->
Transformation Governance
        ->
Product Portfolio Intelligence
        ->
Enterprise Strategy Planning
        ->
M&A Decision Studio
        ->
Board Intelligence
        ->
Enterprise Decision Operating System
```

---

## Expansion Wedges

| Wedge | Buyer | Why It Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation Governance | Chief Transformation Officer | Same decision rigor applied to transformation initiatives |
| Product Portfolio Intelligence | Chief Product Officer | Same tradeoff and prioritization logic for product bets |
| Enterprise Strategy Planning | Chief Strategy Officer | Annual and quarterly planning decisions |
| M&A Decision Studio | Corporate Development | High-stakes decisions with evidence, assumptions, risks, scenarios |
| Board Intelligence | CEO / Corporate Secretary | Executive and board-level decision preparation |
| Enterprise Risk Intelligence | Risk / Operations | Decision traceability and scenario evaluation for strategic risks |

---

## Expansion Mechanics

Atlas expands through:

- More decision templates
- More workspaces
- More business units
- More institutional memory
- More integrations
- More executive reporting
- More governance controls

---

## Expansion Guardrails

Do not expand into a new wedge unless:

- It uses the Decision object model.
- It benefits from Institutional Reasoning Engine.
- It strengthens institutional memory.
- It shares the same governance and traceability model.
- It can be explained as part of Enterprise Decision Operating System.

---

## Decision

Freeze this expansion strategy as the platform growth path for Atlas.
