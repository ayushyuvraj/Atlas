## Red Team Perspectives

### Product Leadership

**Challenge:** The business strategy could overreach beyond the MVP.

**Response:** Layer 10 keeps the first wedge focused on Strategic Portfolio and Investment Decision Support, while treating other wedges as expansion.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Enterprise Buyer

**Challenge:** Decision quality may still sound abstract.

**Response:** The commercial narrative sells concrete outcomes: portfolio governance, capital allocation discipline, reduced rework, and decision traceability.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### CFO

**Challenge:** ROI is directional and not yet quantified with customer data.

**Response:** Layer 10 labels the ROI model as a framework and carries numeric validation into later validation work.

**Status:** Accepted with caveat.

---

### Procurement

**Challenge:** Enterprise procurement may require security, compliance, and vendor-risk detail beyond this layer.

**Response:** Layer 10 defines procurement path. Security overview and procurement-ready collateral should be refined during Launch and Validation.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

### Customer Success

**Challenge:** Adoption could fail if Atlas is seen as extra process.

**Response:** Layer 10 uses a pilot-led rollout tied to an existing decision forum rather than a broad process replacement.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Sales

**Challenge:** Generic AI tools may be perceived as cheaper alternatives.

**Response:** Sales enablement differentiates Atlas by decision lifecycle, governance, traceability, and institutional memory.

**Status:** Addressed.

---

### Skeptical Critic

**Challenge:** Pricing and ROI are not externally validated.

**Response:** Layer 10 explicitly presents pricing as a hypothesis and avoids unsupported price-point claims.

**Status:** Accepted with caveat.
