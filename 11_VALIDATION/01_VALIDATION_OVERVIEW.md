# Validation Overview

## Document Status

**Layer:** 11 - Validation
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Review of Layers 2-10 against the frozen Flagship Program sequence.
**Purpose:** Challenge the Atlas product direction before build work begins.

---

## Validation Thesis

Atlas has a coherent product strategy, customer model, decision-science foundation, product system, PRD, UX direction, technical architecture, and business strategy.

However, several assumptions remain unvalidated in the real market.

Layer 11 exists to separate:

- What is internally coherent
- What is technically credible
- What is commercially plausible
- What still requires external validation

---

## Validation Scope

Layer 11 reviews:

- Product direction
- User experience
- Technical architecture
- AI behavior
- Security and governance
- Executive value
- Competitive differentiation
- Commercial assumptions
- Risks
- Capability and evidence gaps

---

## Validation Standard

An artifact passes validation when it is:

- Consistent with frozen strategy
- Clear enough for the next layer
- Honest about assumptions
- Not overclaiming external proof
- Strong enough to proceed into build planning

This layer does not claim market validation. It creates a structured validation baseline and identifies what must be tested next.
