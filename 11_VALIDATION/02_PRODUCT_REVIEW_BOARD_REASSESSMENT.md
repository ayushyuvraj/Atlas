# Product Review Board Reassessment

## Document Status

**Layer:** 11 - Validation
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Cross-layer review of Product Strategy, Customer Intelligence, Decision Science, Product System Design, Product Definition, Experience Design, Technical Architecture, and Business Strategy.

---

## Review Question

Does Atlas still represent a coherent and buildable enterprise AI product after all major product-definition layers?

---

## Assessment

| Area | Verdict | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem clarity | Pass | Strategic decision fragmentation is consistently defined across layers |
| Customer clarity | Pass with caveat | ICP and personas are clear but still need external validation |
| Product focus | Pass | Initial wedge remains Strategic Portfolio and Investment Decision Support |
| Differentiation | Pass | Institutional Reasoning Engine and Decision Workspace create clear distinction from generic AI tools |
| MVP scope | Pass with caveat | Scope is narrowed, but build planning must avoid implementing all lifecycle states deeply |
| UX clarity | Pass | Progressive disclosure addresses complexity risk at specification level |
| Technical credibility | Pass | Architecture is credible and appropriately staged |
| Commercial logic | Pass with caveat | Business strategy is plausible but pricing and ROI are unvalidated |

---

## Product Review Findings

### Strengths

- Strong chain from problem to product architecture.
- Clear product object: Decision.
- Clear hero surface: Decision Workspace.
- AI is central but not treated as autonomous authority.
- Commercial wedge is concrete enough to demo.

### Weaknesses

- Customer validation is still pending.
- No prototype has been tested.
- Pricing and ROI are hypotheses.
- Technical architecture is not implemented.
- Product may still feel complex without strong UX execution.

---

## Recommendation

Proceed to build planning only if the MVP is constrained to a focused decision workflow:

1. Create Decision Workspace.
2. Add context and evidence.
3. Run structured reasoning.
4. Review executive recommendation.
5. Commit Decision Record.

Do not build the full platform vision in the first MVP.

---

## Decision

Product Review Board approves Atlas to proceed toward build planning with documented validation caveats.
