# Executive Review

## Document Status

**Layer:** 11 - Validation
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Review of product strategy, customer intelligence, business strategy, and demo narrative.
**Purpose:** Validate whether Atlas can be explained to an executive buyer or sponsor.

---

## Review Question

Would an executive understand why Atlas matters?

---

## Executive Summary

Atlas helps enterprises improve strategic portfolio and investment decisions by turning decision preparation, reasoning, recommendation, and final rationale into a structured and reusable workflow.

The product is not a dashboard, chatbot, or document repository.

It is a Decision Workspace that helps leadership teams make decisions with clearer evidence, explicit assumptions, visible tradeoffs, and preserved institutional memory.

---

## Executive Value Test

| Executive Concern | Atlas Response |
| --- | --- |
| Are we funding the right initiatives? | Structured scenario and tradeoff comparison |
| Why are decisions taking so long? | Guided context assembly and recommendation workflow |
| Why do teams disagree? | Explicit assumptions, risks, and competing perspectives |
| Can we trust the recommendation? | Evidence, confidence, alternatives, and traceability |
| What did we decide and why? | Decision Record |
| How do we avoid repeating mistakes? | Institutional Memory |

---

## Executive Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| This sounds like another tool | Atlas upgrades an existing decision forum rather than replacing workflow |
| AI cannot make strategic decisions | Atlas does not make final decisions; humans remain accountable |
| We already have dashboards | Dashboards show what happened; Atlas structures what to decide |
| This may slow us down | MVP success should measure decision cycle time and review cycles |

---

## Executive Readiness

Atlas is executive-readable if the demo shows:

1. A concrete strategic decision.
2. Evidence and assumptions gathered in one workspace.
3. Scenarios and tradeoffs made explicit.
4. AI recommendation with confidence and open questions.
5. Human decision commitment.
6. Decision Record preserved for future reuse.

---

## Decision

Executive Review approves the narrative with the requirement that the demo remain concrete and workflow-led.
