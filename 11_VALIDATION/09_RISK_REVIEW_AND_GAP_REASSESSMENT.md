## Highest-Priority Risks Before Build

### Risk 1 - MVP Scope Creep

The product vision is broad. The build must stay focused on one demo path.

Mitigation:

- Build one strategic investment decision workflow first.
- Do not implement all future lifecycle states deeply.

---

### Risk 2 - Lack of External Validation

Many assumptions are reconstructed from prior reasoning and still need real-world validation.

Mitigation:

- Clearly label assumptions.
- Create customer interview guide and validation plan.
- Use expert or proxy feedback where direct buyer access is limited.

---

### Risk 3 - AI Output Quality

AI recommendations may be generic without strong structure and evaluation.

Mitigation:

- Use structured inputs and outputs.
- Implement evaluation rubric.
- Test with representative decision scenarios.

---

### Risk 4 - UX Density

The product could feel complex or heavy.

Mitigation:

- Preserve progressive disclosure.
- Build executive-first recommendation view.
- Keep analyst detail available but not overwhelming.

---

### Risk 5 - Commercial Assumptions

Pricing, ROI, and procurement path are hypotheses.

Mitigation:

- Test with buyer interviews.
- Build ROI calculator or worksheet later.
- Keep pricing as directional until validated.
