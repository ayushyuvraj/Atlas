## Red Team Findings

### Product

**Finding:** The product direction is coherent and ready for build planning.

**Remaining concern:** MVP must stay narrow.

---

### Customer

**Finding:** ICP and personas are coherent.

**Remaining concern:** Customer evidence is not yet externally validated.

---

### UX

**Finding:** Progressive disclosure is the right experience model.

**Remaining concern:** No clickable prototype or usability testing yet.

---

### Engineering

**Finding:** Architecture is credible and appropriately staged.

**Remaining concern:** Implementation stack and build plan are still open.

---

### AI

**Finding:** AI is central, structured, and evaluable.

**Remaining concern:** Evaluation framework must become an actual test harness later.

---

### Security

**Finding:** Security and access model are credible for MVP.

**Remaining concern:** Production hardening remains future work.

---

### Business

**Finding:** Business strategy is plausible and enterprise-oriented.

**Remaining concern:** Pricing, ROI, and procurement assumptions remain unvalidated.

---

## Go / No-Go Recommendation

**Recommendation:** Go to Layer 12 Build planning.

Conditions:

1. Build one narrow strategic investment decision workflow.
2. Keep unvalidated assumptions visible.
3. Implement evaluation for AI outputs.
4. Avoid broad platform expansion during MVP.
5. Preserve public-safe documentation and traceability.
