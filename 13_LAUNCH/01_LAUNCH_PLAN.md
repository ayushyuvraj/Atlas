# Launch Plan

## Document Status

**Layer:** 13 - Launch
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layers 3-12.
**Purpose:** Define the launch package required for someone to understand and evaluate Atlas without a live walkthrough.

---

## Launch Objective

Create a public-facing product package for Atlas that explains:

- What problem Atlas solves.
- Who it is for.
- How the MVP works.
- What the Institutional Reasoning Engine demonstrates.
- What is included in the current MVP.
- What remains a future production capability.

The launch should make Atlas understandable as a serious enterprise AI product concept and working MVP, while avoiding unsupported claims about customer adoption, production deployment, or live AI integration.

---

## Launch Principle

The launch language must be professional, product-led, and public-safe.

Atlas should be presented as:

> A working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System that helps strategy and portfolio teams run structured, AI-assisted strategic investment decisions.

Atlas should not be presented as:

- A production enterprise deployment.
- A live customer-validated product.
- An autonomous decision-making system.
- A tool that replaces executives.
- A claim about personal career motivation.

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare launch page and product messaging.
2. Connect launch messaging to the local MVP.
3. Create demo script and product tour.
4. Create FAQ, release notes, security overview, and architecture overview.
5. Create sales and executive presentation narratives.
6. Red Team the launch package for credibility, clarity, and unsupported claims.
7. Decide whether to proceed to public hosting and Layer 14 professional narrative work.

---

## Success Criteria

Layer 13 succeeds when:

- The product can be understood without the creator in the room.
- The MVP has a clear demo narrative.
- The launch page explains the product in customer language.
- The launch materials are credible and public-safe.
- Security, architecture, and limitations are transparent.
- The launch package is ready to support Layer 14 public presence and professional narrative work.
