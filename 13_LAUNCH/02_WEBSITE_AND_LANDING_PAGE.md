**Purpose:** Define the product website and landing page narrative for Atlas.

---

## Landing Page Objective

The landing page should help a product, enterprise, AI, or executive reviewer understand Atlas in under two minutes.

It should answer:

- What is Atlas?
- What problem does it solve?
- Who is it for?
- How does the MVP work?
- Why is it different from dashboards, chatbots, or search?
- What can be evaluated today?

---

## Primary Headline

**Run strategic investment decisions with institutional reasoning.**

---

## Supporting Copy

Atlas is a working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System. It helps strategy and portfolio teams structure high-stakes investment decisions, inspect evidence and assumptions, compare tradeoffs, and capture decision rationale as reusable institutional memory.

---

## Core Sections

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

Introduce Atlas as a decision workspace for strategic investment committees.

Key message:

> Strategic decisions should not disappear into slides, meetings, and scattered notes.

### 2. Problem

Purpose:

Explain that enterprises struggle with fragmented reasoning, not only fragmented information.

### 3. Product Workflow

Purpose:

Show the workflow:

```text
Frame -> Reason -> Recommend -> Commit -> Learn
```

### 4. Institutional Reasoning Engine

Purpose:

Explain the reasoning modules:

- Context
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Perspectives
- Scenarios
- Tradeoffs
- Risks
- Recommendation
- Learning

### 5. MVP Demo

Purpose:

Link to the local/demo app and explain what can be evaluated.

### 6. Transparency

Purpose:

Clarify limitations:

- Demo data only
- Deterministic reasoning for first MVP
- No production enterprise integration
- No autonomous decision-making
