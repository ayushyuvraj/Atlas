# Product Demo

## Document Status

**Layer:** 13 - Launch
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Source:** Derived from Layer 12 MVP and Layer 7 Product Definition.
**Purpose:** Define what the Atlas MVP demo should show.

---

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate how Atlas helps an enterprise strategy team run a strategic investment decision with structured institutional reasoning.

The demo should show:

- The Decision Workspace as the product center.
- Reasoning modules as the AI-native capability.
- Human accountability at the commitment step.
- Decision Record creation as institutional memory.

---

## Demo Scenario

An executive committee has limited budget and multiple competing initiatives.

The team must decide:

> Which strategic initiatives should receive funding this cycle?

The MVP uses demo data for competing initiatives, evidence, assumptions, scenarios, risks, and recommendation outputs.

---

## Demo Flow

1. Open Atlas dashboard.
2. Review the active strategic investment decision.
3. Enter the Decision Workspace.
4. Run or review the Institutional Reasoning Engine.
5. Inspect assumptions, perspectives, scenarios, tradeoffs, and risks.
6. Review the Executive Recommendation.
7. Show alternatives and open questions.
8. Commit the Decision Record.
9. Review institutional learning.

---

## Demo Success Criteria

The viewer should understand:

- Atlas is not a chatbot.
- The product structures an enterprise decision lifecycle.
- The AI layer improves reasoning, not executive authority.
- The Decision Record turns rationale into reusable organizational memory.
- The MVP is scoped, credible, and extensible.
