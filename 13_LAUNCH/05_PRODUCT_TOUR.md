# Product Tour

## Document Status

**Layer:** 13 - Launch
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Explain the MVP product experience as a guided tour.

---

## Tour 1 - Home

The home screen shows the active strategic decision, budget, owner, deadline, and initiatives under review.

Purpose:

Help the user understand what decision is active and why it matters.

---

## Tour 2 - Decision Workspace

The Decision Workspace is the hero experience.

It contains:

- Decision objective
- Owner and sponsor
- Budget
- Lifecycle state
- Reasoning module tabs
- Recommendation actions

Purpose:

Give the team one place to prepare and inspect a strategic decision.

---

## Tour 3 - Reasoning Modules

The reasoning modules break the decision into inspectable product objects.

Modules include:

- Context
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Perspectives
- Scenarios
- Tradeoffs
- Risks

Purpose:

Make reasoning structured and challengeable.

---

## Tour 4 - Executive Recommendation

The recommendation view provides concise executive output first, then inspectable detail.

It includes:

- Recommendation
- Confidence
- Rationale
- Alternatives
- Open questions
- Reasoning trace

Purpose:

Support executive review without hiding uncertainty.

---

## Tour 5 - Decision Record

The Decision Record captures the final human-approved decision.

It includes:

- Final decision
- Owner
- Approver
- Success metrics
- Accepted risks
- Review date
- Institutional learning summary

Purpose:

Turn the decision into reusable organizational memory.
