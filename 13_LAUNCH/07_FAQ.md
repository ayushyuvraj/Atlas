# FAQ

## Document Status

**Layer:** 13 - Launch
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Answer common questions about the Atlas MVP.

---

## What is Atlas?

Atlas is a working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System. It helps strategy and portfolio teams structure high-stakes strategic investment decisions.

---

## Is Atlas a chatbot?

No.

Atlas is organized around a Decision Workspace and structured reasoning modules. It uses AI-style reasoning to support a decision lifecycle rather than offering a generic chat interface.

---

## What problem does Atlas solve?

Atlas addresses fragmented enterprise reasoning.

Strategic decisions often happen across slides, spreadsheets, meetings, and disconnected notes. Atlas helps teams capture context, assumptions, evidence, tradeoffs, risks, recommendations, and final rationale in one structured workspace.

---

## Does Atlas make decisions automatically?

No.

Atlas supports human decision-making. It does not approve, reject, or commit decisions autonomously.

---

## What does the MVP include?

The MVP includes:

- Active decision dashboard
- Decision Workspace
- Reasoning modules
- Executive Recommendation
- Decision Record
- Institutional learning summary

---

## Does the MVP use real customer data?

No.

The MVP uses demo data only.

---

## Does the MVP use live AI model calls?

No.

The first MVP uses deterministic reasoning modules to demonstrate the product workflow reliably. The architecture is designed so future versions can connect the same module structure to live model-backed reasoning.

---

## Who is the target user?

The primary user is a strategy, portfolio, transformation, or product strategy team preparing executive investment decisions.

---

## What makes Atlas different?

Atlas is differentiated by its focus on institutional reasoning:

- Explicit assumptions
- Multiple perspectives
- Scenario comparison
- Tradeoff visibility
- Decision records
- Learning from prior decisions

---

## Is Atlas production-ready?

No.

The current version is a working MVP for product demonstration and evaluation. Production capabilities such as authentication, integrations, persistence, live AI orchestration, and enterprise deployment remain future work.
