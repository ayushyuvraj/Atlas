---

## Slide 1 - Title

**Atlas**

Enterprise Decision Operating System for strategic investment decisions.

---

## Slide 2 - The Problem

Strategic decisions are prepared across fragmented systems:

- Slides
- Spreadsheets
- Emails
- Dashboards
- Meetings
- Informal alignment conversations

The reasoning behind decisions is rarely structured or preserved.

---

## Slide 3 - Why This Matters

Poorly structured decisions create:

- Slower executive alignment
- Repeated debates
- Weak assumption visibility
- Lost rationale
- Lower confidence in investment choices
- Limited institutional learning

---

## Slide 4 - Atlas

Atlas gives strategy and portfolio teams a structured Decision Workspace for high-stakes investment decisions.

---

## Slide 5 - Workflow

```text
Frame -> Reason -> Recommend -> Commit -> Learn
```

---

## Slide 6 - Institutional Reasoning Engine

The Institutional Reasoning Engine surfaces:

- Context
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Perspectives
- Scenarios
- Tradeoffs
- Risks
- Recommendation
- Learning

---

## Slide 7 - Initial Use Case

Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support.

Example:

> Which initiatives should receive funding this cycle?

---

## Slide 8 - Value Proposition

Atlas helps enterprise teams:

- Prepare better executive decisions
- Reduce decision cycle time
- Improve assumption visibility
- Make tradeoffs explicit
- Preserve rationale
- Build institutional memory
