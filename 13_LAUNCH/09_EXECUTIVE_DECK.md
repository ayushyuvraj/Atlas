# Executive Deck

## Document Status

**Layer:** 13 - Launch
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Provide an executive-level narrative for Atlas.

---

## Slide 1 - Strategic Decisions Need Infrastructure

Enterprises have systems for customers, finances, projects, and data.

They rarely have a system for how strategic decisions are reasoned through, committed, reviewed, and learned from.

---

## Slide 2 - The Executive Problem

High-stakes decisions often suffer from:

- Conflicting assumptions
- Fragmented evidence
- Siloed perspectives
- Unclear tradeoffs
- Lost rationale
- Weak learning loops

---

## Slide 3 - Atlas

Atlas is a working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System.

It helps leadership teams make strategic investment decisions more transparent, rigorous, and reusable.

---

## Slide 4 - Product Philosophy

AI does not replace executive judgment.

AI makes executive judgment more structured, explainable, and repeatable.

---

## Slide 5 - The Decision Workspace

Atlas centers the product around a Decision Workspace.

Every decision captures:

- Context
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Alternatives
- Tradeoffs
- Risks
- Recommendation
- Final record
- Learning

---

## Slide 6 - Initial Wedge

Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support.

This wedge is valuable because investment committees already exist, the pain is recurring, and the decision stakes are high.

---

## Slide 7 - What the MVP Proves

The MVP proves:

- Product workflow
- Reasoning structure
- Executive recommendation format
- Human commitment model
- Decision memory concept

---

## Slide 8 - What Remains Future Work

Future production work includes:

- Live AI provider integration
- Enterprise identity
- Persistent backend
- System integrations
- Multi-user collaboration
- Customer validation

---

## Slide 9 - Platform Vision

Atlas can expand from one strategic investment workflow into a broader Enterprise Decision Operating System.
