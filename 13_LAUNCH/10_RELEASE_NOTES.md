## Release

**Atlas MVP v0.1**

---

## Summary

Atlas MVP v0.1 introduces a working local product experience for strategic portfolio and investment decision support.

The MVP demonstrates how a strategy team can use a Decision Workspace to inspect structured reasoning, generate an executive recommendation, and commit a Decision Record.

---

## Included

- Enterprise app shell
- Active decision dashboard
- Decision Workspace
- Demo strategic investment decision data
- Deterministic Institutional Reasoning Engine
- Reasoning module tabs
- Executive Recommendation
- Decision Record commitment flow
- Institutional learning summary
- Automated reasoning tests
- Local run instructions
