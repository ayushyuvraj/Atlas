---

## Layer Objective

Convert the Atlas product program, MVP, and launch package into a coherent professional narrative that can be understood by product leaders, AI leaders, recruiters, hiring managers, and enterprise technology stakeholders.

The narrative must be product-led, evidence-based, and public-safe.

---

## Narrative Principle

The public narrative should not describe private career motivation.

It should describe:

- The enterprise problem.
- The product thesis.
- The product development process.
- The MVP.
- The evidence trail.
- The strategic and technical decisions behind the product.
- The remaining limitations and future roadmap.

The work should demonstrate product leadership through the quality of artifacts rather than through inflated claims.

## Public-Safe Boundary

Public materials may say:

- Atlas is a working MVP.
- The repository documents an end-to-end product program.
- The product demonstrates AI-native decision support for strategic investment workflows.
- The reasoning engine is deterministic in the first MVP.
- Customer and market claims remain hypotheses unless validated.

Public materials must not say:

- The product is production-deployed.
- The product has live enterprise customers.
- The MVP uses live AI model orchestration.
- The product replaces executive judgment.
- The repository exists for private career-positioning reasons.
