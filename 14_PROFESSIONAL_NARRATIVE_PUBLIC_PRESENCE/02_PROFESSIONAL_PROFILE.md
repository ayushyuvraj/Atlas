## Short Bio

I work at the intersection of enterprise AI, product strategy, and decision intelligence. My work focuses on designing AI-native products that help organizations reason more clearly, make better strategic decisions, and preserve institutional knowledge over time.

Atlas is a working MVP and product program exploring that thesis through an Enterprise Decision Operating System for strategic investment decisions.

---

## Medium Bio

I focus on enterprise AI product strategy: identifying high-value organizational problems, translating them into AI-native product systems, and building the product, architecture, GTM, and launch artifacts needed to make those systems credible.

My current flagship project is Atlas, a working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System. Atlas helps strategy and portfolio teams run structured strategic investment decisions by surfacing evidence, assumptions, perspectives, scenarios, tradeoffs, risks, recommendations, and decision records.

The project includes not only the MVP, but also the product strategy, customer intelligence, decision science model, PRD, UX, technical architecture, business strategy, validation, launch package, and evidence trail behind the product.

## Public Narrative Guardrails

Use language that emphasizes:

- Product judgment
- Enterprise problem selection
- AI-native workflow design
- Responsible AI boundaries
- Evidence-based product development
- Technical and commercial fluency

Avoid language that implies:

- Unverified customer traction
- Production deployment
- Unsupported seniority claims
- Autonomous AI decision-making
- Private career strategy
