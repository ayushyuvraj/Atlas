## About Section Draft

I work at the intersection of enterprise AI, product strategy, and decision intelligence.

My focus is on AI-native products that help complex organizations reason better, make higher-quality strategic decisions, and preserve institutional knowledge over time.

My current flagship project is Atlas, a working MVP for an Enterprise Decision Operating System. Atlas explores how strategy and portfolio teams can structure high-stakes investment decisions using evidence, assumptions, multiple perspectives, scenarios, tradeoffs, risks, recommendations, and reusable Decision Records.

The project is built as a full product program: opportunity discovery, product strategy, customer intelligence, decision science, PRD, UX, technical architecture, business strategy, validation, MVP build, and launch package.

I write about enterprise AI, decision intelligence, product judgment, responsible AI workflows, and the operating systems organizations need for the AI era.

- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Give the reader a useful framework or takeaway.
