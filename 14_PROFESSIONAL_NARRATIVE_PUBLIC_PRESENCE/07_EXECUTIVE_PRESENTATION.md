## Slide 1 - Atlas

Enterprise Decision Operating System for strategic investment decisions.

---

## Slide 2 - The Problem

Enterprises have systems of record for customers, finances, projects, documents, and analytics.

They rarely have a system of record for strategic reasoning.

---

## Slide 3 - Why It Matters

When reasoning is fragmented:

- Assumptions stay hidden.
- Tradeoffs remain unclear.
- Executive alignment slows.
- Rationale disappears after decisions.
- Organizations repeat the same mistakes.

---

## Slide 4 - Product Thesis

Strategic decisions should become living enterprise assets.

---

## Slide 5 - Atlas Product Concept

Atlas provides a Decision Workspace for high-stakes strategic decisions.

It structures:

- Context
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Perspectives
- Scenarios
- Tradeoffs
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Decision Records
- Learning

---

## Slide 6 - Initial Wedge

Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support.

Example:

> Which initiatives should receive funding this cycle?

---

## Slide 7 - Institutional Reasoning Engine

The Institutional Reasoning Engine makes reasoning inspectable, challengeable, and reusable.

It does not replace human judgment.

It improves the quality of preparation and review.
