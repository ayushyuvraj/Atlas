## Narrative

Atlas is a working MVP and product program for an Enterprise Decision Operating System.

The project explores a simple but important product thesis:

> Strategic decisions should become reusable institutional assets.

Large enterprises often prepare high-stakes decisions across slides, spreadsheets, dashboards, meetings, and informal notes. The information may exist, but the reasoning behind the decision is often scattered, implicit, or lost after the meeting.

Atlas addresses that gap through a Decision Workspace for strategic portfolio and investment decisions.

The MVP demonstrates how a strategy team can:

- Review a strategic investment decision.
- Inspect evidence and assumptions.
- Compare multiple perspectives and scenarios.
- Surface tradeoffs and risks.
- Generate an executive recommendation.
- Commit a human-approved Decision Record.
- Preserve institutional learning.

The repository documents the product journey end to end:

- Opportunity discovery
- Product strategy
- Customer intelligence
- Decision science
- Product system design
- Product definition
- Experience design
- Technical architecture
- Business strategy
- Validation
- Build
- Launch

The current MVP uses demo data and deterministic reasoning modules. It is not a production deployment and does not claim live customer validation.

Its purpose is to demonstrate a serious product concept, product operating discipline, and a credible path toward AI-assisted institutional reasoning for enterprise decision-making.
