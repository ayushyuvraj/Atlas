# Future Wedge Portfolio

## Document Status

**Layer:** 15 - Platform Expansion
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Define future Atlas product wedges and their relationship to the core platform.

---

## Wedge Portfolio

| Wedge | Primary Buyer | Primary User | Decision Type | Why It Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation Command Center | Chief Transformation Officer | Transformation Leads | Transformation funding and risk decisions | Uses the same portfolio governance and execution-risk reasoning |
| Product Portfolio Intelligence | Chief Product Officer | Product Strategy Teams | Product investment and roadmap tradeoffs | Extends strategic investment logic into product portfolio prioritization |
| Executive Strategy Workspace | Chief Strategy Officer | Strategy Teams | Annual and quarterly strategy decisions | Uses the same context, scenario, and tradeoff reasoning for strategy planning |
| Enterprise Risk Intelligence | Chief Risk Officer / COO | Risk and Operations Teams | Strategic and operational risk decisions | Uses decision traceability and scenario evaluation for risk posture |
| M&A Decision Studio | Corporate Development | Corp Dev and Integration Teams | Acquisition, divestiture, and integration decisions | High-stakes decisions with evidence, assumptions, scenarios, and risks |
| Board Intelligence | CEO / Corporate Secretary | Executive Office | Board-level strategic decisions | Converts board preparation into structured decision records |
| Enterprise AI Chief of Staff | CEO / CIO / CSO | Executive and Strategy Teams | Cross-functional executive decision support | Uses institutional memory and decision context across workflows |
| Decision Memory Platform | CIO / CSO | Enterprise Knowledge and Strategy Teams | Historical decision reuse | Turns accumulated decisions into a searchable and reusable memory layer |

---

## Wedge Prioritization

### Near-Term

1. Transformation Command Center
2. Product Portfolio Intelligence
3. Executive Strategy Workspace

These are closest to the MVP because they reuse portfolio, prioritization, scenario, and governance patterns.

### Mid-Term

4. Enterprise Risk Intelligence
5. M&A Decision Studio
6. Board Intelligence

These require stronger governance, security, and workflow specificity.

### Long-Term

7. Enterprise AI Chief of Staff
8. Decision Memory Platform

These require richer institutional memory, integrations, and cross-workflow learning.

---

## Non-Wedges

Atlas should avoid:

- Generic chat assistants
- Meeting summarizers
- Generic document search
- Task management clones
- Standalone BI dashboards
- Workflow automation disconnected from decision quality

These areas may be integrations, but they should not become the product identity.
