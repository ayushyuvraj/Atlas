# Expansion Sequencing

## Document Status

**Layer:** 15 - Platform Expansion
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Define the recommended order for expanding Atlas beyond the first MVP.

---

## Sequencing Logic

Expansion should be based on:

- Reuse of current MVP assets
- Buyer proximity
- Workflow similarity
- Decision frequency
- Implementation feasibility
- Strategic platform value
- Institutional memory compounding

---

## Recommended Sequence

### Phase 1 - Strengthen the Current Wedge

Improve Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support.

Add:

- Persistent Decision Records
- Better visual polish
- Live AI adapter
- User feedback capture
- Public deployment
- Customer validation

Rationale:

Do not expand before the first wedge is credible.

---

### Phase 2 - Transformation Command Center

Extend the same decision model into enterprise transformation governance.

Why next:

- Transformation portfolios resemble investment portfolios.
- Buyers overlap with current ICP.
- Risks, assumptions, dependencies, and execution tradeoffs are central.

---

### Phase 3 - Product Portfolio Intelligence

Extend Atlas into product investment and roadmap prioritization.

Why next:

- Product leaders face similar prioritization and tradeoff problems.
- The workflow demonstrates cross-industry applicability.
- The product can reuse scenarios, evidence, and recommendation patterns.

---

### Phase 4 - Executive Strategy Workspace

Extend Atlas into annual and quarterly strategy planning.

Why next:

- Strategy teams are core users.
- The workflow increases executive visibility.
- Institutional memory becomes more valuable across planning cycles.

---

### Phase 5 - Specialized High-Stakes Wedges

Add:

- M&A Decision Studio
- Enterprise Risk Intelligence
- Board Intelligence

Why later:

These workflows require stronger governance, integrations, confidentiality, and domain specificity.

---

### Phase 6 - Decision Memory Platform

After multiple workflows exist, elevate institutional memory into a platform product.

Why later:

Decision memory becomes more valuable only after enough decisions, outcomes, and learning artifacts accumulate.

---

## Expansion Guardrail

Atlas should not expand into a new wedge until the current wedge has:

- Clear demo
- Clear user value
- Stable workflow
- Documented limitations
- Defined success metric
- Plausible buyer narrative
