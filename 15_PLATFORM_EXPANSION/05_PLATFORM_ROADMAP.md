# Platform Roadmap

## Document Status

**Layer:** 15 - Platform Expansion
**Version:** v1.0
**Status:** Frozen
**Purpose:** Provide a platform roadmap for Atlas beyond the current MVP.

---

## Roadmap Horizon 1 - MVP Stabilization

### Goal

Make the current strategic investment workflow more credible and reviewable.

### Work

- Public deployed demo
- Visual QA and polish
- Persistent Decision Records
- Basic backend
- Live AI adapter
- User feedback capture
- Demo video
- Customer interview plan

---

## Roadmap Horizon 2 - Workflow Expansion

### Goal

Expand from one decision template to multiple related decision workflows.

### Candidate Templates

- Transformation portfolio review
- Product portfolio review
- Annual strategy planning
- Capital allocation review
- AI initiative prioritization

---

## Roadmap Horizon 3 - Enterprise Platform Foundation

### Goal

Turn Atlas into a reusable enterprise platform.

### Work

- Enterprise identity
- Role-based access
- Audit logging
- Decision history
- Institutional memory search
- Integrations with documents and collaboration systems
- Evaluation pipeline
- Admin controls

---

## Roadmap Horizon 4 - Decision Intelligence Layer

### Goal

Use accumulated decision records and outcomes to improve future reasoning.

### Work

- Outcome reviews
- Assumption calibration
- Decision quality analytics
- Pattern detection
- Recommendation quality evaluation
- Cross-workflow learning

---

## Roadmap Horizon 5 - Enterprise Decision Operating System

### Goal

Support multiple strategic decision workflows across business units.

### Work

- Multi-workflow decision templates
- Cross-functional governance
- Executive dashboards
- Board-ready decision summaries
- Platform APIs
- Integration marketplace

---

## Roadmap Decision

The next practical roadmap step is not immediate wedge expansion.

The next practical step is MVP stabilization:

1. Public deployment
2. Visual polish
3. Persistence
4. Live AI adapter
5. Customer validation
