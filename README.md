# Atlas

Atlas is a public product operating repository for an Enterprise Decision Operating System.

The project explores how large organizations can make better strategic portfolio and investment decisions using AI-assisted institutional reasoning.

## Product Direction

| Area | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Platform vision | Enterprise Decision Operating System |
| Category | Enterprise Intelligence Platform |
| Core product | AI-Native Multi-Agent Decision Studio |
| Initial wedge | Strategic Portfolio & Investment Decision Support |
| Core innovation | Institutional Reasoning Engine |
| Management framework | Institutional Reasoning Management |

## Why This Exists

Enterprise strategic decisions are often fragmented across meetings, slides, spreadsheets, dashboards, and undocumented context.

Atlas investigates a product thesis:

> Strategic decisions should become reusable institutional assets, not isolated meeting outcomes.

The repository documents the product reasoning behind that thesis, including opportunity discovery, capability mapping, customer hypotheses, competitive deconstruction, prioritization, risks, assumptions, and Red Team reviews.

## Repository Structure

```text
00_PROGRAM/
    Program governance, dashboard, decision log, assumptions, risks, changelog, and public release policy.
```

Additional layers will be added in sequence as the product program progresses.

## Integrity Notes

Some artifacts are reconstructed from prior working sessions and are clearly labeled as such.

Customer pain, market sizing, and AI timing claims are treated as hypotheses until validated through external research, customer interviews, or expert review.

Local-only source inputs, private notes, and publication-prep scaffolding are intentionally excluded from the public repository.
